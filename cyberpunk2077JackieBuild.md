# The Jackie Build (the V-Jackie save)

**Retired** (November 16, 2026). The V-Jackie save is dropped: there is one save, and every fight on screen is V's footage. This file is kept for reference only. Its Update 2.5 perk details are also in `cyberpunk2077PerksAndSkills.md`.

The build for the second save, played in Jackie's style to record his combat clips. It starts from a dual-pistol "Jackie Welles" build idea and is changed to fit **the base game with Update 2.5, without Phantom Liberty**.

---

## Why it fits Jackie

- **The game gave Jackie this playstyle.** Update 2.5 (October 22, 2026) added dual-wielding, and its perks are named after him: **Heywood Hurricane**, **Triple Tequila** and **Splash of Love**. "A splash of love" is the last ingredient in the Jackie Welles drink at the Afterlife.
- **Jackie is the muscle.** He fights loud and in the open, at close to mid range, with no stealth and no netrunning. V holds fire until the other party escalates; Jackie goes in first.

## What changes without Phantom Liberty

| In the idea | Without Phantom Liberty |
|---|---|
| Level 60 | **Level 50.** 71 attribute points in all, not 81. |
| Reflexes 20, Body 18 | Can't afford both. See the spread below. |
| Chrome Compressor (Operating System) | **Phantom Liberty only.** Use the Militech "Apogee" Sandevistan. |
| Relic perks | Phantom Liberty only. None are used. |

**Corrections to the idea**
- **Handle Wrap doesn't reduce recoil.** It's for throwables. Immovable Force and Shock Absorber are the recoil options.
- **Chitin may be Phantom Liberty.** One source places it with the Dogtown ripperdoc.
- **Epimorphic Skeleton and Cellular Adapter** weren't found in the sources. Check in game.
- **La Chingona Dorada's iconic effect**, as the sources describe it: every headshot temporarily raises Crit Damage and Burn chance. The idea's claim that a headshot also adds a round to the other pistol wasn't found.

**At character creation** every attribute starts at 3, with 7 points to place and a cap of 6. To have dual-wielding as early as possible: Cool 6, Body 6, Reflexes 4. Three more levels into Cool reach Cool 9, and Double Trouble with it.

**Order after that:** Cool 9, Body 4 (already there), Reflexes 9, Technical Ability 9, then the 15s, then Technical Ability 20, then Cool to 18 or 20, depending on the spread.

## Weapons

**La Chingona Dorada** is a pair of gold Power pistols built on the Nue.

**The catch: they only exist after Jackie dies.** They sit on his ofrenda in the side job Heroes, after The Heist.
- To get them, send Jackie's body to his family in The Heist and finish Heroes.
- Then leave El Coyote Cojo, skip 24 hours, and take them from the altar.
- Sending his body anywhere else locks them out. (One guide says Viktor still works.)

So Jackie can't carry his own guns before The Heist.

**Decided: record before The Heist, with stand-ins.** They aren't gold, and the footage keeps the pre-Heist state.
- **Which stand-ins isn't decided:** two Nues, or Dying Night (free from Wilson in The Gun) with a Nue.
- **What this means for the build:** the clips are recorded inside Watson before The Heist, so V-Jackie won't be at level 50 when they are. How far the build gets by then is open.

**Not taken:** finishing The Heist and Heroes on the V-Jackie save first, to record the Watson gigs with the real pair. It would have put Act 2's state in the footage.

**What Jackie carries in the game itself is unconfirmed.** One player says he uses Omaha pistols in the pre-Heist missions, and that the altar pistols are Nues he never used. Check in the footage.

**Backup:** a third-slot shotgun or machete for point-blank fights, from whatever is available at that point.

## The look

- **No mods.** Update 2.5 added mod support; this playthrough doesn't use it.
- **The game is first person.** V-Jackie's outfit only shows in third person (driving), in mirrors and in photo mode.
- **His exact jacket isn't in the game.** Build the look in the Wardrobe from what vendors sell. Update 2.5 added five outfit sets; check whether any suits him.
- **Body:** match Jackie as closely as character creation allows. His look changes over the montage: a shaved head and no cyberware at first, then hair, a beard and chrome.
- **Ride:** Jackie's Arch is only given at the end of Heroes, so it's also after The Heist.
