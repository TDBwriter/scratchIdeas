# V, Jackie and T-Bug: What the Game Actually Shows

Who the three are in *Cyberpunk 2077* itself, before the playthrough adds anything. Only sourced facts. Where the game is silent or the sources disagree, the entry says so or stays blank. Scenes come from the pre-Heist planning log (`cyberpunk2077PreHeistPlanningLog.pdf`), which was built from the sources listed at the end.

---

## V (male, Streetkid)

**The facts**
- **Name:** Vincent. The game withholds it at first; it's confirmed in the main job "Automatic Love," after The Heist.
- **Age:** 27. A developer clarified V is "27 not 22 or whatever."
- **Voice:** Gavin Drea (male V).
- **From:** Night City.
- **Job:** an edgerunner, a mercenary with cyberware.
- **Lifepath:** Streetkid. It sets V's background and the opening mission, The Streetkid, which starts in El Coyote Cojo in Heywood. Streetkid-only dialogue options turn up in later conversations.

**What the game fixes and what it leaves to the player**
- **Fixed:** the story beats: the Heist, the Relic, Dex's betrayal, Johnny Silverhand in V's head, limited time to live.
- **Player's choice:** gender, voice, looks, cyberware, background, and most of V's attitude, through dialogue choices. CD Projekt kept V first-person on purpose so the player defines V; the game doesn't give V a set personality.

**V before Jackie (The Streetkid)**
- V turns up at the bar with a broken nose and says someone tried a mugging.
- Pepe the bartender owes Kirk money, due tomorrow. **V offers to help.**
- V takes Kirk's job to steal a Rayfield Aerondight and gets a ride from Padre and Marcus Barros. Padre gives V his contact.
- At the car: Jackie, gun drawn. The NCPD arrests both of them, and a cop knocks them out after the owner, Kaoru Fujioka, talks to the police.
- They wake on the street together and **decide to get lunch.**

## Jackie Welles

**The facts**
- **Born:** 2047 (listed as 2047-2077).
- **Voice:** Jason Hightower.
- **From:** Heywood.
- **Gang:** a former Valentino. One source says he had to leave once his over-protective mother found out.
- **Heritage:** Latino, judging by the Spanish he swears in. The wiki hedges this with "seems."
- **Class:** Solo. A mercenary, a gun for hire.
- **Family:** Mama Welles. She runs the memorial at El Coyote Cojo after his death.
- **People around him:** Misty runs Misty's Esoterica, where he waits for V in The Ride. She is at his garage and his ofrenda in Heroes. Viktor Vektor, the ripperdoc, sits beside V at the ofrenda.
- **Ambition:** the big league. "The gig of a lifetime" that sets him up for good. At the Afterlife: "Afterlife's a sign for us."
- **Gear:** a car (V drives it in The Ripperdoc), a motorcycle (Jackie's Arch), and a gun, La Chingona Dorada. The bike and the gun are only seen in Heroes, after The Heist.
- **The wiki's words:** "a dedicated family man and accomplished solo."

**How he meets V, by lifepath:** in every lifepath Jackie starts V's career as a merc. For a Streetkid, they first meet trying to steal the same car.

**Jackie and V on screen, before The Heist**

| Mission | What happens between them |
|---|---|
| The Streetkid | Jackie pulls a gun on V over the same car. Both are arrested and knocked out. They wake up together and go for lunch. |
| Six-month montage | About 1 minute 20 seconds of their first six months working together, across about 15 places. The developers say it was always planned as a montage. |
| Practice Makes Perfect | Jackie hands V a Militech training shard. T-Bug takes the training over. |
| The Rescue | The first crew job on screen. Jackie and V split the first two scavengers between them and dump a body. They drive home through a police cordon, and V talks the officer into letting them through. |
| The Ripperdoc | Jackie calls V in the morning from a food stall downstairs and mentions another job. V takes his car keys and drives them both to Viktor's. Misty is at the front desk. |
| The Ride | Jackie, at Misty's, tells V where to find Dex. After Dex's pitch, V calls Jackie and briefs him. |
| The Pickup | Jackie explains the job: the Maelstrom took a Militech bot, the Flathead, and getting it back is a test before the bigger job. At the negotiation Jackie is nervous, a ganger pulls a gun on him, and V can tell him to sit down. |
| The Heist | V and Jackie talk with Claire at the Afterlife. After Dex's briefing they are alone together. The chip's case breaks in the jump, so on Evelyn's instruction Jackie puts the chip in his own head. He is badly hurt in the escape. In the Delamain cab, V comforts him and can ask the cab to go to a ripperdoc. Jackie gives V the chip and dies. V chooses where his body goes and touches his shoulder before getting out. |

**After The Heist (Heroes)**
- **Trigger:** Heroes needs the main job Playing for Time, and Jackie's body sent to his family. If his body went to Viktor instead, V just picks up a package from Mama Welles with the bike keys in it.
- **The memorial:** Mama Welles texts V and holds an ofrenda (a memorial altar) for Jackie at El Coyote Cojo.
- **Jackie's garage:** V talks to Misty about his things there. From the next room V can take a book, a basketball, a belt and a bottle of tequila. Guides disagree on how many can go on the ofrenda.
- **The ceremony:** V can persuade Misty to come or leave her be, and sits next to Viktor.
- **Afterwards:** Mama Welles offers Jackie's Arch. V can say no. La Chingona Dorada can be taken from the altar.

## T-Bug

**The facts**
- **Real name:** none given in the sources found.
- **Voice:** Cynthia Kaye McWilliams, per IMDb's credits. That page couldn't be opened directly to confirm.
- **Class:** Netrunner. One wiki lists her location as Westbrook, with no specific place.
- **Background, from the wiki:**
  - "a textbook example of how solos and netrunners really aren't all that different," and she is "well-aware of this fact."
  - A specialist who can be picky about her contracts.
  - Unlike more reckless netrunners, she "intends to sail into retirement age."
- **Role:** V and Jackie work with her now and then on small gigs before Dex's job.
- **Death:** killed in the net during the Konpeki Plaza lockdown. Sources differ on how it's described: Arasaka's netrunners kill her in the security sweep, or the Arasaka subnet detects her and fries her brain remotely. The game moves past it quickly; one commentary notes the player only learns she's dead when V mentions it later.

**T-Bug and the crew on screen, before The Heist**

| Mission | What happens |
|---|---|
| Practice Makes Perfect | Her first appearance. She reveals she's altered Jackie's Militech training and takes over the scenarios: shooting, hacking, stealth, combat. |
| The Rescue | On comms. In the elevator she briefs V and Jackie on the client, Sandra Dorsett. |
| The Gift (side job) | She sends V a job: pick up the PING quickhack from Yoko at Kabuki Market and try it on a camera. |
| The Information | On V's third dive into the braindance, V brings T-Bug in. Judy resists; T-Bug: "Relax, Judy. Everything'll be fine." Jackie isn't in this job. |
| The Heist | At Dex's briefing in the Afterlife, then leaves with Dex. At Konpeki check-in she helps with a receptionist who threatens to call Taki-san. Killed in the net during the lockdown. |

**After The Heist:** no messages, memorial or job about her turned up in the sources found. Left blank.

## What the game doesn't show

This is the room the playthrough has to fill. Nothing below is in the game.

- **V and Jackie's six months.** The montage is the only record: about 80 seconds, no dialogue documented here. What jobs they did, and how lunch became a partnership, is unseen.
- **How T-Bug joined them.** She first appears already working with Jackie (taking over his training shard). How Jackie or V found her, and how long she'd known Jackie, isn't shown.
- **Jackie and T-Bug together, without V.** No scene found.
- **T-Bug's life.** No real name, home, history or reason for the handle found. Only her outlook: picky about contracts, planning to retire.
- **T-Bug after her death.** Nothing found: no funeral, no message, no one asking after her.
- **Jackie's past.** The Valentinos and his mother's reaction are mentioned. The rest (his childhood, how he met Misty and Viktor) wasn't confirmed in the sources found.
- **V's past before the bar.** The Streetkid lifepath opens with a broken nose and a story about a mugging; what came before isn't shown here.
- **Jackie and T-Bug in the jobs they skip.** Jackie isn't in The Information; T-Bug isn't in The Ripperdoc, The Ride or The Pickup. In the game, V does those without them.
