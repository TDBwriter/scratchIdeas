# CHEAP TRUTH: The FF:06:B5 Clues

Ten clues, numbered 0 to 9, that walk a player through the FF:06:B5 hunt inside the game, in order. Each is a riddle that stands alone, written in the zine's voice. They hide in an issue's extra sections: the Top Ten, the interviews, Gripe Time, the copyright joke, the colophon.

Everything here comes from players' published guides. Nothing has been checked in the game yet (see "Check in game before using").

---

## The hunt, plainly

This is the answer key. The clues below are written from it.

| # | What the player does | Where | Source |

| 4 | Notes the scratched-out number on the cabinet (reported as 240891). Plays Arasaka Tower 3D. On Floor 52, enters the pink "MRPHY" high-score room, kills the enemy, touches the golden artifact. | The church | Prima Games (via search); The Nerd Stash |

**Beyond the game.** One more step needs *The Witcher 3*: a glyph mural in the Destroyed Bastion in Velen decodes into passwords for the church's mainframe, which unlock a last cutscene of Polyhistor, Tyromanta and V searching for a "watcher." Who the watcher is remains unsolved (Screen Rant).

**A shortcut exists.** Players report the truck can be had by reading the church computer at 4 AM, touching the arcade cabinet once, and going straight to the mattress. Guides say that route shows a mysterious figure where the full route shows a golden cube.

## The clues

Each has the riddle, what it leads to, and where it could hide. The hiding places are options.

### 0. The color

> There is one color this sheet can't print. Six characters, three pairs: FF, 06, B5. Mix it and you get the pink of a bruise under neon. Somebody cut those six characters into stone in this city and never said why. I went looking.

- **Leads to:** the code, and the idea that it's carved somewhere.
- **Could hide in:** the copyright joke; the masthead; a colophon slogan.
- **When:** any issue. It's the only clue that needs no travel.

### 1. The statue

> Corpo Plaza, northwest of the big roundabout. A statue with more arms than it needs holds a sword in two hands and a ball in a third. Monks kneel to it. The suits walk past. I read what's cut across its front, and so can you.

- **Leads to:** the statue.
- **Could hide in:** a Top Ten X on the statue; an interview aside.

### 2. The snake

> Start at the Medeski pumps and walk into the junk. Keep northwest until a crane stands over you. There's a red box there the size of a train car. In the trash beside it a laptop is still awake, showing a snake that eats its own tail, over and over, to nobody.

- **Leads to:** the laptop in the trash pile.
- **Could hide in:** a Top Ten X on the trash pile; Gripe Time; the Interview with the Grandfather, whose subject ends up at a landfill.

### 3. The church at four

> Out past the city, in the Biotechnica flats, the protein farms keep a church with a blue steeple and no congregation. The door used to be locked. Inside, the altar is server racks, a computer and an arcade machine. The computer keeps one office hour. Read every message on it at four in the morning. Two names sign them: Polyhistor and Tyromanta.

- **Leads to:** the church and the 4 AM read.
- **Could hide in:** a Top Ten X on the church; an interview aside.

### 4. The pink room

> Somebody scratched a number off the side of the arcade cabinet, and didn't scratch hard enough. Six digits. Keep them. Then play. The first floor is called 52. There's a pink room on it with a name on the score board that lost its vowels: MRPHY. Kill what's in there and touch the gold.

- **Leads to:** the six digits and the first secret room.
- **Could hide in:** the Interview with the Saint, whose subject is a Murphy.

### 5. The wait

> There's a second room of high scores on 52. Kill what's in it. Then do the hardest thing there is in a shooter: nothing. Watch the clock. When it falls under 272, a little padlock shows on your screen. Now you can leave.

- **Leads to:** the padlock.
- **Could hide in:** Gripe Time, as a rant about waiting; the Interview with the Professional ("I was on time").

### 6. The walk down

> Go back to the brown walls and the bright paintings, to the dead end with numbers on it. Walk the cabinet's six digits with your feet, in order: 2, 4, 0, 8, 9, 1. You get a key for every right step. Then take the elevator. It goes ten floors lower than the building does. Down there is a maze on a timer, and the walls say IT SEES YOU. There's a code in a square that leads to a game of noughts and crosses nobody can win. Finish the maze anyway.

- **Leads to:** Floor -10.
- **Could hide in:** the Top Ten itself: its entries are numbered 0 to 9, so the six digits can be six entries read in that order.

### 7. The seven numbers

> When the game is over, look at the church again. The server racks have grown keypads. Seven numbers, left to right, and they only take them before four in the morning: 327670. 318308. 527766. 727862. 240. 108850. 204217. Get them all in, and the computer has a new file.

- **Leads to:** the new file.
- **Could hide in:** numbers scattered through one issue: prices in an interview, the colophon's timestamp, a Top Ten's verdicts.

### 8. The third file

> Open the third file on the church computer. It doesn't tell you anything. It shows you where to stand.

- **Leads to:** the map location.
- **Could hide in:** one line of an editorial.

### 9. The mattress

> Past the Wraith camp, where the billboards face the road, turn left into the desert and walk until you find a mattress nobody would sleep on. Stand on it at four in the morning. Touch nothing. Sometime before six you'll start to cough, and then you'll stop for a while. When you come round there's a truck the size of a house beside you, and somebody has left you something to read.

- **Leads to:** the cutscene and the Demiurge.
- **Could hide in:** a Top Ten X on the mattress; a closing copyright joke.

### Beyond the game (optional)

> The last door doesn't open from this side. Its key is painted on a ruined wall in another world, an older one, where they still settle things with swords.

- **Leads to:** *The Witcher 3*. It can't be followed inside *Cyberpunk 2077*.

## What a Top Ten can mark

The hunt has four places an X can sit on:

| Place | Clue |
|---|---|
| The statue, Corpo Plaza | 1 |
| The trash pile, near Medeski Fuel Station | 2 |
| The church, Biotechnica Flats | 3 |
| The mattress, past Wraith Camp | 9 |

A Top Ten can mark up to three spots, so two issues are enough to put an X on all four.

**For the "ten finds" format,** the hunt doesn't supply ten places. Four are listed above. The other statues, if there are four, would add up to three more.

---

## What limits where clues can go

- **Before The Heist, only clue 0 can be followed.** Every place in clues 1 to 9 is outside Watson: Corpo Plaza and the Badlands. An "Edgerunners 0" issue can print a later clue, and its Top Ten lines can point out of Watson at one of these places, but V can't go and photograph them yet.
- **Far targets need care.** The church and the mattress are a long way from the city. A small error in a photo's position moves a distant crossing a long way.
- **Order.** The clues only work in order from 3 to 9. Clues 0, 1 and 2 are separate finds and can run in any order before 3.
- **Standing alone.** Each riddle above can be read without the others. None says "last issue."
- **Real facts in the mouth of a narrator.** These are things anyone in Night City could see, so the eyewitness voice holds.

## Check in game before using

1. **That the hunt still works on the current patch.** One player reported the truck not appearing on patch 2.02. Guides from 2025 describe it working.
2. **The seven keypad codes.** They come from a 2023 guide.
3. **The scratched number on the cabinet,** and that it reads 240891.
4. **The clock value.** One guide says under 272 seconds; other reports say 271 or 270.
5. **The times:** the 4 AM read, the keypads only before 4 AM, and how long the wait on the mattress is. Guides give 5:40 AM, "about 6 AM" and "about 55 in-game minutes."
6. **Whether Phantom Liberty is needed.** The guides only say Update 2.0.
7. **How many statues there are, and where.**
8. **Which cutscene each route shows.**

## Still open

- Which issue carries which clue.
- Whether the *Witcher 3* step is printed.
- Whether the zine gives the seven codes outright or hides them. No published source found explains how players derived them, so a riddle can't lead a reader to work them out.
- Whether the zine mentions the shortcut.
