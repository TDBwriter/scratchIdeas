# Interview prep: Iwinski, Kicinski and Nielubowicz, praising trickle-down economics

**Status:** living, real people, executives of the company that makes the game.
**What this is:** a fourth-wall easter egg. It breaks test F1 and section 7 on purpose.
**The caricature:** three bumbling buffoons. Humorously cynical.
**Names:** the zine's ASCII rule drops the diacritics: Iwinski, Kicinski.

## What's on the record
- **Marcin Iwiński** co-founded CD Projekt in 1994 with **Michał Kiciński**. Iwiński left executive work and became Co-Chair of the Supervisory Board in January 2025.
- **"Kiciński" is ambiguous.** Michał is the co-founder. **Adam Kiciński** was the longtime joint CEO, left the management board at the end of 2024, and is the other Supervisory Board Co-Chair. The events below involve Adam. The draft uses the surname only. **Pick one before publishing.**
- **Piotr Nielubowicz** is the Chief Financial Officer and a member of the management board.
- **Public events the jokes lean on:**
  - The company's reply to worries about online features: "We leave greed to others."
  - On the release date: the game would be "ready when it's done."
  - The game launched in December 2020 in a poor state on consoles.
  - On January 13, 2021, Iwiński released an apology video. He said the console version "did not meet the quality standard" they wanted, and that he and the leadership were "deeply sorry." A roadmap of patches came with it.

## Keeping it fair
- **The target is an economic theory and a corporate way of talking.** The buffoonery is physical and obviously made up: a shared lanyard, a champagne tower, morgue drawers.
- **The only real events are the public ones above,** and they're used as echoes, not accusations. The piece claims nothing about the three men's private conduct, pay or intentions.
- **It says it's invented,** in the zine's own voice, in the last line.

Sources: CD Projekt, Management Board and Supervisory Board pages; PAP Biznes, report 2/2025; PC Gamer, "We leave greed to others"; Engadget, Creative Bloq and Know Your Meme on the January 2021 apology video and roadmap.

**Invented for the joke:** everything they say and do.

## Draft interview

## INTERVIEW WITH THE MANAGEMENT

We decided to go over everyone's head. Above the corps, above the Night family, above Maximum Mike himself, somebody must actually be in charge of this city, and we asked the Afterlife's bottom shelf to send them down. Never mind how.

To our surprise we got three, all alive. They arrived by the service elevator, having first tried two of the old morgue drawers. They wore matching fleece vests and shared one lanyard, which meant they moved as a unit. Mr. Iwinski pushed a catering cart with a champagne tower on it. Mr. Kicinski carried a laminated roadmap. Mr. Nielubowicz carried a calculator and was already using it.

CT: Gentlemen, this is a great pleasure. You're from --

IWINSKI: Management.

CT: Of what?

KICINSKI: (gesturing at everything, and knocking over a stool) This.

CT: The bar?

NIELUBOWICZ: The bar, the city, the weather. It's one line item.

CT: Preem. Can we get you anything?

IWINSKI: No, no. We brought a demonstration. (He wheels the tower forward. It is nine glasses high. The top glass is a bucket.) Trickle-down economics! The most beautiful idea in the world. You pour the wealth in at the top --

(He pours. The bucket fills. Nothing else happens.)

CT: It's not reaching the other glasses.

KICINSKI: (consulting the roadmap) It's not supposed to yet. The lower glasses are scheduled for a later update.

CT: When?

KICINSKI: When it's done.

NIELUBOWICZ: Which is a date. We have announced the date. (calculating) We are moving the date.

CT: The bucket's nearly full, though.

IWINSKI: That's how you know it's working! You concentrate the wealth where it's safest. Up here. Then gravity does the rest. It's physics. You can't argue with physics.

CT: Gravity's had a while.

IWINSKI: (swapping the bucket for a larger bucket) We listened to the feedback.

CT: Who's the bucket? In the city, we mean.

NIELUBOWICZ: That's a forward-looking question.

KICINSKI: We want to be very clear. We leave greed to others.

CT: Which others?

KICINSKI: (checks the roadmap; turns it over; checks the back) It doesn't say.

CT: People down here say the corps took everything, and nothing came down but the ads.

IWINSKI: The ads are the trickle! Light is a form of wealth. You're standing in it. For free!

NIELUBOWICZ: (counting the glasses) Nine. (recounts) Eleven. We've had a very strong year.

(Mr. Kicinski, reaching for the roadmap, pulls the lanyard. All three go into the cart. The tower comes down on our notes.)

IWINSKI: (from the floor, producing a small yellow card) We would like to apologize. We are deeply sorry that the floor did not meet the quality standard we set for our champagne.

KICINSKI: A fix for the floor is on the roadmap.

CT: (wringing out the notes) Gentlemen, may we say something? You built this whole city as a warning about -- well, about management. This zine is the same warning. So in a way we're on the same side. We're colleagues.

IWINSKI: (touched) We love community content. Who do we talk to about the license?

CT: Nobody. It's not copyrighted.

(A long silence. Mr. Nielubowicz enters this into the calculator. The calculator makes a sound.)

NIELUBOWICZ: At all?

CT: At all.

KICINSKI: Then how does it trickle?

CT: It doesn't. It gets passed hand to hand.

IWINSKI: (horrified) Sideways?

NIELUBOWICZ: That's not an economy. That's a rumor.

(The Management leaves through a morgue drawer, on the second attempt. They tipped the bartender one glass from the bottom of the tower. It was empty, but they assured her it was scheduled. None of this happened. The gentlemen are real, alive, and were not consulted. We leave accuracy to others.)
