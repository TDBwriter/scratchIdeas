# Cyberpunk 2077 Narrative Playthrough: Cohesion Plan

Audit of every project file on the branch `cyberpunk2077-narrative-playthroughs`, as of November 10, 2026 (2026:314), commit `d999b6b`. Brought up to date on November 16, 2026 (2026:320). The companion file `cyberpunk2077ProjectReference.md` says what the project is. This file says what has to happen for the documents to agree with each other and be usable for production.

## What the audit found

1. **The playthrough itself is not written down.** The seed file `cyberpunk2077Playthroughs.md` was still an empty template (since filled in). The difficulty, the build, the weapons, the two saves, the ending and the YouTube pipeline exist only in the planning conversation. The reference file now records them, marked as such.
2. **Thirteen decisions are holding up everything downstream.** Five of them block recording. They're listed below with their options. (Several have since been decided; see the next section. Recording has since started anyway.)
3. **The documents contradict each other in 22 places.** Most trace back to those decisions. Seven are mechanical fixes.
4. **The working tools were not in the repo** (since copied into `tools/`). The planning log's source, the issue checker and the original zine's text live in a session scratch folder that disappears when this session ends. Without the source, the 141-page planning log can't be regenerated or updated.
5. **Nothing exists past The Heist.** The planning log, the crew scenes and the arcs all stop there, and about 21 of the projected 28-29 issues come after it.
6. **The Streetkid's issue (drafted as [0]) passes every scripted check on structure and voice, and fails on content:** its Top Ten is all placeholders, it has no photos and no puzzle clue. (Its Gripe Time was off-topic under the old rule. The rule has since been lifted.)

## Decided since the audit

- **V is always neutral** in every file (Q1).
- **V is a pacifist with a rule of force,** from the first job (Q2). V is deeply conscientious about the appropriate level of violence, doesn't start it and doesn't take life wantonly. V answers escalation by the other party with lethal force, goes loud in self-defense with a gun V carries, and keeps the tomahawk for silenced kills if the situation calls for one. Backup to Jackie.
- **After The Heist, V stops holding back:** quicker to escalate and readier to kill.
- **Who fights on screen:** first decided gig by gig, Jackie (the V-Jackie save) or V. Replaced: the V-Jackie save is dropped, so every fight is V's footage.
- **Main jobs are carried by their scenes,** not by four moments. The four-moments rule covers side content. The Streetkid's own block of four moments is removed.
- **How the crew leaves a sealed Watson is never explained.**
- **"Without leaving bodies" and "Nobody gets hurt. That's my cut." both stand** beside V's rule of force.
- **Which gun V carries: left blank** on purpose.
- **Regina's first call stays in The Ride,** where the game plays it. How her gigs begin in the story is open.
- **The montage's place: left blank** on purpose.
- **The two lines scene 1 left behind** go into an early side-gig scene. Which one isn't picked.
- **V-Jackie's clips were to be recorded before The Heist with stand-in pistols.** Replaced: the save is dropped.
- **The serial is written in the zine's voice throughout.** The samizdat note's closing "something to do" line is cut.
- **V's build waits for The Heist.** All attributes stay at 3 until then; V chromes up in response to it.
- **After The Heist V is a one-person powerhouse,** with quickhacks and guns, and hacks openly as V.
- **The crew's new lines are text only** (part of Q8).
- **"The final issue follows the money to V" is cut** (part of Q10).
- **The earlier build plan is the build after The Heist.** Before it, V has only the cyberware the game installs.
- **An issue uses only what's reachable** at that point in the game (Q5).
- **The reader isn't told which two Top Ten places make a line** (part of Q7).

- **An issue runs 1,200 to 2,000 words** (part of Q10; test A9).
- **"NOT COPYRIGHTED" stays three times per issue** (part of Q10).

- **Recording has started:** hours of footage, with Steam's automatic recording. Played: The Streetkid, Practice Makes Perfect, The Rescue, The Ripperdoc, then NCPD jobs. Viktor is paid back, out of recovered weapons and ammo. Steam's automatic recording overwrites old footage unless it's saved.
- **Every entry gets a full video of its own,** each assault site included: 80 before The Heist. **Game overs, surprises and dumb deaths go into YouTube Shorts** (part of Q9). Changed on November 18: a few deaths stay in the full video too, and each mission's Short is a skate-video part with its deaths in it (`cyberpunk2077ShortsTreatment.md`).
- **Each Short's sequence** (November 19): the previous game over as the cold open, a title card over the loading screen, the run with slow parts sped up, skit breaks, the coup de grace, and V moving off into the city. Flashback slams of an enemy who killed V before. The title card carries V's whole profile. Takedowns in Shorts are V's, as played. What a Short opens on when V didn't die: left blank.
- **Not decided:** how a full video's narration treats a death the crew's story says didn't happen.
- **"Not leveling" means every attribute and perk point is banked.** Skills rise by use; equipment is gathered where possible.
- **One save. The V-Jackie save is dropped,** and its build file is retired. Every fight on screen is V's footage. Each fight is credited to Jackie or to V in the edit, decided as it's cut.
- **The 7 creation points were left unspent.** Every attribute is at 3.
- **The planning log keeps its checklist order.** The files no longer call it play order.
- **Housekeeping, all on your yes:** the tools are in `tools/`; Update 2.5's dual-wield perks are in the perks reference; the recording checklist is saved with notes; the playthrough file is filled in.
- **Issues are numbered in story order** (part of Q4). The Streetkid is no longer issue [0], and its number isn't set.
- **What the zine publishes during the side gigs: left blank** on purpose (part of Q6).
- **DISPATCH stays as written:** a crew member under an epithet, a stated exception to the voice rule (part of Q11).
- **The interview rule is widened** to any legend from Cyberpunk 2020 or Cyberpunk RED who is dead, believed dead, or whose fate is unknown by 2077 (part of Q11).
- **The player does every hack; the story credits T-Bug.** The edit covers each hack with a photo V appears to have snapped. T-Bug is a script kiddie who only uses tricks seen in gameplay (Q3).
- **The Streetkid is the first mission toward The Heist.** "Edgerunners 0" is every other job that can show the crew working together. The order of the gigs inside it is undecided (part of Q4). See "Two orders" below.
- **The Top Ten is "X marks the spot":** two lines through pairs of photo locations cross at a marked spot, up to three spots. Or all ten locations are themselves finds (part of Q7).
- **The extra sections carry the hidden clues.** A ten-clue list is drafted in `Cheap-Truth-screamsheet-CLUES.md` (part of Q7).
- **The two real-people interviews are fourth-wall easter eggs** and break the "no real names" rule on purpose (part of Q8).

- **Two silos (Q13).** The zine lives in `Cheap-Truth-screamsheet/` and the crew's storyline in `Crew-Dialogue/`, each standing on its own, with a file per crew member. This also fixed X4 (the anonymity line), X12 (the issue file's number) and X18 (the style guide's title), and started the issue ledger (O3).

- **Every gig carries the four moments, no matter how small:** one development each for V, Jackie and T-Bug, and one bonding moment, all tied to the gig's subject. Each of the 24 assault sites is a gig of its own: 72 gigs and 288 moments.
- **Every supporting character who works with V before The Heist is in scope,** with unequal time, to serve the crew's development.
- **The game's own lines are repeated wherever possible.**
- **The zine's serial is numbered,** and each part still stands alone. "Cops' Cash" is the final reveal, in the serial and in the crew's NCPD arc: the cycle of money and violence that corporate rule keeps turning.

- **Two orders.** The Streetkid is played first, because the game requires it. The videos tell every side gig first, then The Streetkid, then the road to The Heist. Practice Makes Perfect, The Rescue and The Ripperdoc are told ahead of the side gigs.
- **No Phantom Liberty.** The playthrough is the base game with Update 2.5, so the level cap is 50 and there are 71 attribute points.
- **The chip:** Jackie slots it after the case is damaged. V slots it after Jackie is shot and dying.
- **The V-Jackie save's build** started from a dual-pistol idea, changed to fit the base game (`cyberpunk2077JackieBuild.md`). Retired with the save.

**New questions these raise**
- **After The Heist, who does the story credit with the hacks?** Answered: V, openly.
- **How is the ending played?** The first plan ended in a solo raid on Arasaka by an "unhinged" V. V now stops holding back after The Heist, which is close to that. The raid itself isn't planned.
- **The game still makes you pick a body and a voice** for V at character creation, even though the files stay neutral.

## 1. Decisions that need you

Ordered by how much each one touches. Each lists the options without a recommendation.

### Blocks recording

**Q1. Who is V on screen? Decided.** V is always neutral in the files. The game still asks for a body and a voice at creation.

**Q2. What does "pacifist" allow, and does it end at The Heist? Decided.** From the first job, V doesn't start violence or take life wantonly, answers escalation with lethal force, goes loud in self-defense with a gun V carries, and keeps the tomahawk for silenced kills if the situation calls for one. After The Heist, V stops holding back. Still open: which gun, and how the solo Arasaka ending is played.

**Q3. Who does the hacking on screen? Decided.** The player performs every hack. The edit covers it with a photo V appears to have snapped, and the story credits T-Bug, who only uses tricks seen in gameplay. After The Heist V hacks openly as V.

**Q4. Which missions get an issue, and in what order?**
- **Decided so far:** two orders. The Streetkid is played first and told after the side gigs. The order of the gigs is undecided. Phantom Liberty is out.
- **Now:** "one issue per main job," numbered from 0 in story order. Still unsettled:
  - The Streetkid is a lifepath mission, not a main job, and it is told after every side gig. Its draft still carries [0] as a placeholder.
  - Nothing is published during the side gigs under the current rule. That gap is left blank for now.
  - Practice Makes Perfect gets its own issue: decided. It is [0], The Rescue [1], The Ripperdoc [2].
  - The Information and The Pickup can be played in either order.
  - Act 2's three branches can be played in any order.
  - Love Like Fire, the interlude played as Johnny, is undecided.
- **Options:** fix one canonical list now / number by the order you actually play / number by act and position (for example 2.07).
- **Touches:** every issue ID after the first change (each ID is printed twice per issue); the puzzle's clue order; the total count.

**Q5. When is an issue published, given what V can reach? Decided:** only what's reachable. Each issue uses places V can reach at that point in the game. The options below are kept for the record.
- **Before:** "published after each main job." But Watson is locked down until The Heist. The Streetkid's issue is set in Heywood, the Afterlife opens at The Heist, and the known FF:06:B5 sites are in Corpo Plaza and the Badlands.
- **Options:**
  - Publish on time, using only what's reachable. The Streetkid issue's Top Ten would have to come from the prologue's fixed route (check in game).
  - Shoot photos on a separate scouting save with the whole map open. That bends the "this playthrough only" rule.
  - Hold issues until their places open. That breaks "after each mission."
- **Touches:** the Top Ten of all 7-8 pre-Heist issues; interview photos; the release schedule.

### Blocks writing issues

**Q6. How is side content assigned to issues?**
- **Now:** undecided. Before The Heist there are 9 side jobs, 22 gigs, 6 cyberpsychos and 14 NCPD entries to place in 7-8 issues.
- **Options:** by play order (it goes in the next main job's issue) / by distance to the main job's location / by theme, assigned by hand in a ledger.
- **Touches:** test E2; Top Ten places; the departments; 51 planning-log entries.

**Q7. How does the FF:06:B5 puzzle run? Partly decided.**
- **Decided:** the clue for the step that needs *The Witcher 3* is printed. The seven keypad codes are not, so the zine alone doesn't finish the hunt. The reader isn't told which two Top Ten places make a line. The extra sections carry the clues. The Top Ten marks up to three spots with crossing lines, or all ten places are finds. Ten clues are drafted in `Cheap-Truth-screamsheet-CLUES.md`.
- **Still open:**
  - which issue carries which clue;
  - how close a line must pass;
  - whether the map is printed.
- **To know:** before The Heist only the first clue can be followed, because every other place in the hunt is outside Watson.
- **Touches:** the Top Ten of every issue; tests E4 and E6; scouting time.

**Q8. Real people, voices and rights.**
- **Real people: decided.** The Pondsmith and CD Projekt interviews are fourth-wall easter eggs and break test F1 on purpose. The Kiciński in the piece is Adam. Neither interview carries a disclaimer.
- **Voices: decided.** The 17 crew scenes (13 numbered, and 4 for The Streetkid) are new dialogue for V, Jackie and T-Bug. They are text only: on-screen text and messages. Nobody speaks them.
- **Rights:** "NOT COPYRIGHTED" can only cover your own text and ASCII art. Footage and photos fall under CD Projekt's fan-content terms, which also govern monetization.
- **Touches:** test F1; every invented scene; the channel's risk.

**Q9. What is an episode, and how does it tie to an issue?**
- **Now:** "each mission will be a zine and an episode." Every entry, each assault site included, is edited into a full video of its own: 80 before The Heist. A few deaths stay in the full video. Each mission also gets a Short of up to 3 minutes, cut as a 90s skate-video part, with that mission's deaths in it.
- **Open:** one episode per issue or several; where the invented scenes sit; whether the video's narrator is the zine's eyewitness "I"; where the zine is published; where the photos are hosted, since every caption links to one.
- **Touches:** test G6; the pipeline; issue length.

### Blocks finishing issues

**Q10. The issue's fixed parts.**
- Length: decided. An issue runs 1,200 to 2,000 words, as the original did (test A9). The Streetkid's draft is about 1,150 with a blank Top Ten.
- What counts as an "article" for the three follow-the-money questions (test B1).
- "NOT COPYRIGHTED" appears three times: top, colophon, bottom. Decided: all three stay.
- The colophon's address, graphics credit and timestamp. They are now blank in The Streetkid's draft; the invented stand-ins are removed. You said you'd describe a fictional date pattern. The game shows no calendar date, so the in-world day can't be read off the screen.
- Decided: the line "the final issue follows the money to V" is cut from the rules.

**Q11. Which departments exist, and who qualifies?**
- **Interview with the Dead: decided.** The rule now admits any legend from Cyberpunk 2020 or Cyberpunk RED who is dead, believed dead, or whose fate is unknown by 2077. That covers the drafts for Trace Santiago, Spider Murphy and Miriam Night.
- **DISPATCH: decided.** It stays as a piece credited to a crew member under an epithet, and is the one stated exception to "never the crew's point of view."
- **STREET ART: decided.** The department is folded into the Top Ten.

**Q12. The build.**
- **Decided:** all of V-media's attributes stay at 3 until The Heist. In response to The Heist, V chromes up, and goes from sidekick to a one-person powerhouse with quickhacks and guns. This replaces the creation spread and "near maximum build by The Heist."
- **What follows from it:** no perks before The Heist, since the first perk tier opens at an attribute of 4. Attribute and perk points are banked through Act 1.
- **Checked:** the 7 creation points were left unspent, so every attribute is at 3.
- **Decided:** the earlier build plan (Intelligence to 20 first, then Reflexes and Cool, with Body and Technical Ability as dump stats; cyberware for stealth, free-running and reconnaissance) is the build after The Heist. Before it, V has only the cyberware the game installs.
- **Still a tension in that build:** brawling leans on Body, and Body is a dump stat.
- Q2 settled the tools: wits, a camera, a tomahawk, fists and a gun V carries. Which gun is blank. The first plan named the DA8 Umbra.
- The level cap is 50 without Phantom Liberty: 71 attribute points for V-media as well.
- Free-running cyberware (a double jump) comes after The Heist. Top Ten "ways up" before it have to work for a body with no added cyberware.
- The Cyberpunk RED sheet that mirrors the build isn't written down.

**Q13. Repo layout. Decided.** Two silos, `Cheap-Truth-screamsheet/` and `Crew-Dialogue/`, each independent. The playthrough files stay at the repo root for now.
