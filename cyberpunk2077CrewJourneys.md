# Crew Journeys: V, Jackie and T-Bug

A hero's journey for each of the three, and one for the crew, told in scenes set during missions and in the moments between them. Built on what the game shows (`cyberpunk2077CrewProfiles.md`).

**V's gender** is left open in the planning files, so the plan can adapt. V is "V" or "V-media," and Jackie calls V "choomie," and sometimes "hermano."

**How to read it**
- **[GAME]** happens in the game. It can't change.
- **[OURS]** is invented for the playthrough: scenes staged in photo mode or with the V-Jackie save, and voices laid over calls and messages in the edit.
- Every [OURS] scene sits between [GAME] beats and never contradicts them. Jackie and T-Bug die in The Heist, and every arc bends toward it.

**Staging**
- **V-media** is the player character.
- **Jackie** is placed with photo mode or played on the V-Jackie save. Photo mode in 2.2 can add characters; check in game that Jackie is one of them.
- **T-Bug** is on remote comms (the phone and holocall screen, with her voice in the edit) in every scene where the game doesn't put her in the room. That means everywhere except the Afterlife.
  - Her skills match what she does in the game (see her section in `cyberpunk2077CrewProfiles.md`): she briefs, directs and links; V does the hands-on hacking.
  - She needs a way into a system. In these scenes, her way into street cameras is V's PING habit, which she taught V in The Gift. That's a stretch of her canon, not a change to it.
  - Whether photo mode can place her is unconfirmed.

---

## The shape

Each journey uses the twelve stages of the hero's journey (Christopher Vogler's version of Joseph Campbell's): Ordinary World, Call, Refusal, Mentor, Threshold, Tests/Allies/Enemies, Approach, Ordeal, Reward, Road Back, Resurrection, Return.

- **V-media's** runs the whole game. The Heist is V's Ordeal; stages 10-12 come after it.
- **Jackie's and T-Bug's** are tragedies. They reach the Ordeal and don't come back. Their Return happens through other people: Jackie's in Heroes [GAME], and T-Bug's only if the playthrough gives her one [OURS].
- **The crew's** journey treats the three as one hero, from strangers to the Heist. Jackie is the muscle (solo), T-Bug the brains (netrunner), V-media the eyes (backup and documentation).

## Scenes

Each scene lists when it happens, where, who's in frame, the shot, the dialogue, and which arcs it moves. Locations marked "pick in game" still need scouting.

### 1. Lunch with a stranger
- **When:** The Streetkid, right after they wake. [GAME: they decide to get lunch]
- **Where:** a food stall in Heywood (pick in game).
- **In frame:** V-media and Jackie.
- **The shot:** two bruised faces over two bowls, the camera on the counter between them.

> **JACKIE:** About the gun. Nothing personal, choomie. I had a guy, you had a guy. Our guys had the same idea.
> **V:** Your guy get you out of the cuffs?
> **JACKIE:** Some cop got us out of the cuffs. With his fist.
> **JACKIE:** So what was in it for you? You don't look like you boost cars for fun.
> **V:** Bartender owes money. Due tomorrow.
> **JACKIE:** And you're his what? Family?
> **V:** I drink there.
> **JACKIE:** You stole a car for the guy who pours your drinks.
> **V:** Tried to.
> **JACKIE:** Hermano. That's the dumbest or the best thing I heard all year.
> *(He nods at the camera.)*
> **JACKIE:** And that? You shoot people with it?
> **V:** Just pictures.
> **JACKIE:** You don't carry?
> **V:** Don't need to.
> **JACKIE:** Everybody in this city needs to.
> **V:** Then somebody should be watching them do it.
> **JACKIE:** *(a beat)* Jackie Welles.
> **V:** V.
> **JACKIE:** Okay, V. Next job I get, you're on it. You watch. I'll carry.

**Moves:** the crew strikes its deal; V-media names the refusal; Jackie picks a partner by heart, not sense.

### 2. Sauce on his chin
- **When:** the six months (the montage).
- **Where:** a rooftop in Watson (pick in game; a candidate Top Ten spot).
- **In frame:** Jackie. V-media behind the camera.
- **The shot:** Jackie against the skyline, eating, not posing.

> **JACKIE:** You gonna take a picture or eat?
> **V:** Both. Hold still.
> **JACKIE:** Make me look like a legend.
> **V:** You look like a guy with sauce on his chin.
> **JACKIE:** Then that's the legend. *(He wipes it off and poses.)* Now?
> **V:** The first one was better.

**Moves:** V-media's first portrait of Jackie; the legend Jackie wants against the man he is. Scene 12 calls back to it.

### 3. She picked us
- **When:** the start of Practice Makes Perfect. [GAME: Jackie hands V a Militech training shard; T-Bug takes the training over]
- **Where:** wherever Practice Makes Perfect starts (check in game).
- **In frame:** V-media and Jackie. T-Bug on the call.

> **JACKIE:** Got you a present. Militech training shard. Real deal.
> **T-BUG:** *(cutting in)* Real deal. Sure. I've been inside it since breakfast.
> **JACKIE:** T-Bug! Say hi to V.
> **T-BUG:** Hi, V. Jackie talks about you too much.
> **V:** You're the netrunner.
> **T-BUG:** I'm the one who checks. Jackie vouches for everyone. I don't.
> **JACKIE:** She's picky.
> **T-BUG:** I'm alive. Same thing. Slot it. Let's see what you've got.

**Moves:** T-Bug's Call (an audition); the crew's Mentor arrives; three of them together for the first time.

### 4. Three minutes out
- **When:** the drive home after The Rescue. [GAME: a police cordon; V talks the officer into letting them through]
- **Where:** the car, at the cordon.
- **In frame:** V-media and Jackie. T-Bug on the call.
- **The shot:** the cordon lights through the windshield.

> **T-BUG:** Trauma Team's got her. Platinum client, platinum service.
> **JACKIE:** And the lady in the first room?
> **T-BUG:** Wasn't a client.
> *(Silence.)*
> **V:** I took her picture.
> **JACKIE:** The dead one? Why?
> **V:** Somebody should know she was there.
> **T-BUG:** Nobody pays to know that, V.
> **V:** I know.
> **T-BUG:** *(a beat)* Send it to me. For the file. I keep files.

**Moves:** V-media's witness theme starts; T-Bug's first crack (she keeps it); Jackie learns why V watches.

### 5. Eat, then eyes
- **When:** the start of The Ripperdoc. [GAME: Jackie calls from the food stall downstairs; V drives them to Viktor's]
- **Where:** the food stall below V's building.
- **In frame:** Jackie eating; V-media arriving.
- **The shot:** Jackie mid-bite under the building's lights.

> **JACKIE:** Sit, choomie. Eat. You don't walk into Vik's on an empty stomach.
> **V:** What's he putting in?
> **JACKIE:** Kiroshis. Best eyes in Watson for the price.
> **V:** I've got a camera.
> **JACKIE:** Now you'll have two. And this one you can't drop off a roof.
> **V:** How much?
> **JACKIE:** Twenty-one large.
> **V:** Jackie.
> **JACKIE:** Pay when the job pays. Vik's good for it. So are you.

**Moves:** Jackie as V-media's mentor; the lens becomes part of V. It sets up the game's choice to pay Viktor now or later.

### 6. Every camera's a window
- **When:** The Gift. [GAME: T-Bug sends V to Yoko for the PING quickhack, to try on a camera]
- **Where:** Kabuki Market, at the camera.
- **In frame:** V-media at the camera. T-Bug on the call.

> **T-BUG:** Ping it.
> **V:** Done.
> **T-BUG:** Now you see everything it's wired to. Every camera on that net lights up.
> **V:** And you?
> **T-BUG:** I see what you light up. You just opened me a door.
> **V:** Cameras are windows.
> **T-BUG:** Most of them face in, V. Remember that when you're standing in front of one.
> **V:** Who's watching me through them?
> **T-BUG:** Today? Me.

**Moves:** T-Bug as V-media's mentor; the witness theme turns to surveillance, which is who watches whom. It sets up her way into cameras in scenes 7 and 8: V pings, she follows.

### 7. Noodles, and a third voice
- **When:** free roam, after The Gift.
- **Where:** a noodle stall in Kabuki Market (pick in game).
- **In frame:** V-media and Jackie on stools. T-Bug on a three-way call, shown on the phone screen.
- **The shot:** two backs at the counter, steam, the phone face-up between the bowls with T-Bug's call on it.

> *(The call chimes. Neither of them called.)*
> **JACKIE:** Did you...
> **V:** No.
> **T-BUG:** You two chew like you're paid by the slurp.
> **JACKIE:** T! Where are you?
> **T-BUG:** Somewhere with better food. *(a beat)* That's a lie. It's protein paste.
> **V:** Come down. Kabuki. We'll save you a stool.
> **T-BUG:** I don't do stools. Stools are how they find you.
> **JACKIE:** Who's they?
> **T-BUG:** Whoever's paying them this week.
> **V:** You called us, though.
> **T-BUG:** You pinged the stall's camera when you sat down. Habit. My habit. I taught you that.
> **V:** So you followed the ping. For a job? Or to check on us?
> *(A pause.)*
> **T-BUG:** The broth's too salty. Get the number four.
> **JACKIE:** *(to the cook)* Number four! *(to the phone)* So what's your number, T? For retirement. How much?
> **T-BUG:** More than this city wants to pay.
> **JACKIE:** And where would you go?
> **T-BUG:** Somewhere with no signal.
> **V:** You'd go crazy in a week.
> **T-BUG:** That's the plan. Go crazy slow. On a porch.
> **JACKIE:** Me, I want a seat at the Afterlife. My name on the wall. A drink with my name on it.
> **T-BUG:** You want them to remember you. I want them to forget me. One of us is going to be disappointed.
> *(V raises the camera at the phone screen.)*
> **V:** Smile.
> **T-BUG:** Point that at me and I'll brick it.
> *(Click.)*
> **T-BUG:** ...Send me that one.

**Moves:** the first time T-Bug calls without a job; Jackie says his want out loud; V-media's camera reaches her. Jackie's line pays off: since Update 1.5 the Afterlife serves a Jackie Welles (vodka, lime juice, ginger beer, "a splash of love"), so after The Heist V can order one.

## Writing more scenes

**Voices**
- **Jackie:** warm, loud, quick to laugh. Spanish in the mix. Calls V "choomie," and "hermano" when it counts (scenes 1 and 11). Food. Mama Welles, Misty, Vik. Legends and the Afterlife. Brave talk, hands that shake.
- **T-Bug:** dry, short sentences. Numbers, cameras, odds. Arrives uninvited on calls. Deflects with retirement jokes. Her care comes out as information.
- **V-media:** quiet and watchful. A photographer's words: frame, light, "hold still." A Streetkid who knows Heywood. Won't carry a gun. Kind without making speeches.

**Rules**
1. **Never contradict a [GAME] beat.** Jackie isn't in The Information, and T-Bug isn't in The Ripperdoc, The Ride or The Pickup, so on those days they only appear on calls before or after.
2. **T-Bug is on remote comms** everywhere except the Afterlife, and her skills match her canon. She briefs, directs and links, and needs a way in to any system. V does the hands-on work.
3. **Every scene moves at least one arc** a step.
4. **Every scene can be staged:** in photo mode, on the V-Jackie save, or as a call.
5. **Keep Jackie's and T-Bug's endings in sight.** Plant what The Heist takes away.
