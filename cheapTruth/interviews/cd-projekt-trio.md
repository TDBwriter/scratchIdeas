## Draft interview

## INTERVIEW: THE PEOPLE UPSTAIRS

*PARODY. This interview never happened. None of these men said any of this. It's a joke about an economic theory, wearing their names.*

The Afterlife doesn't serve the people who built the city. They don't drink here. They own the building the bar is under. So I didn't summon them. I asked Claire for the most expensive thing on the menu and they arrived to see who was paying.

Three men in good jackets, from the real world, wherever that is. They looked at the Afterlife the way you'd look at a model of a city you'd built, if the model had started charging rent.

**CT:** Gentlemen. Thanks for coming down.

**IWINSKI:** Down is where the magic happens. That's the whole philosophy.

**CT:** Which philosophy?

**KICINSKI:** Trickle-down. It's very simple. Wealth goes to the top first, because the top is where it's safest. Then, naturally, it trickles.

**CT:** Trickles where?

**NIELUBOWICZ:** Down.

**CT:** How far down?

**NIELUBOWICZ:** *(checking a tablet)* That's a forward-looking statement.

**IWINSKI:** Look at this city. Look at those towers. Every one of them is a promise. Up there, the money is concentrated, focused, efficient. And down here, you get the light that comes off it.

**CT:** That's the ads.

**IWINSKI:** It's illumination.

**CT:** When does the money arrive down here?

**KICINSKI:** It's already arriving. In the form of opportunity. You have the opportunity to look up.

**CT:** Night City has a word for that. Arasaka.

**NIELUBOWICZ:** A fictional company.

**KICINSKI:** Purely fictional.

**IWINSKI:** We would never.

**CT:** Never what?

**IWINSKI:** *(smiling)* Anything.

**CT:** This zine says corporations run the world more than governments do.

**KICINSKI:** That's not a criticism. That's a business model.

**NIELUBOWICZ:** And we've had a very strong year.

**CT:** Who paid for the strong year?

**NIELUBOWICZ:** Everybody, eventually. That's the beauty of it. Everyone pays, and then everyone benefits.

**CT:** In that order?

**NIELUBOWICZ:** In that order.

**CT:** You built this city. Doesn't it bother you what's in it?

**IWINSKI:** We built a warning. A beautiful, very successful warning.

**KICINSKI:** You should see the warning's margins.

**IWINSKI:** *(picking up a copy of the zine)* Now, this. This is wonderful. Real community content. We love this kind of thing. Who do we talk to about licensing?

**CT:** Nobody. It's not copyrighted.

*(A long silence.)*

**NIELUBOWICZ:** At all?

**CT:** At all.

**KICINSKI:** Then how does it trickle?

**CT:** It doesn't trickle. It gets passed hand to hand.

**NIELUBOWICZ:** *(closing the tablet)* That's not an economy. That's a rumor.

They left together, for a call. On the bar they left a tip of exactly zero, folded very neatly, for the bartender to look up at.
