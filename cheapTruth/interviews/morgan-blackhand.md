# Interview prep: Morgan Blackhand

**Status by 2077:** missing since 2023. Believed dead; not confirmed.
**Fits the department:** yes, as the "Heard you were dead" echo of the original's #17.

## What canon says
- The top Solo of Cyberpunk 2020: the core rulebook. *Solo of Fortune 2* ranks him first and credits him with *The Enforcer's Handbook*. Mike Pondsmith created him.
- Lost his right arm as a soldier in the Central American wars. Became a mercenary, mostly for Militech.
- In 2019 he stopped an attempt to kidnap Kerry Eurodyne and took all five kidnappers alive.
- In the Fourth Corporate War he led Militech special operations in the ocean war. He tried, and failed, to stop an Arasaka team from killing Rache Bartmoss.
- In August 2023 he joined the Militech strike on Arasaka Tower. On the roof, he stayed behind to fight Adam Smasher so his team could get out. The tower was then destroyed. His fate is unknown.
- Cyberpunk RED repeats rumors: that he reported to the US president, that he worked as a secret government asset, that he was seen in the cities that recovered first.
- In 2077 he has no drink at the Afterlife, because nobody is sure he's dead. A news story about a Japantown massacre quotes a survivor describing a portly man with a black cyberarm. A fan wiki says Jackie names him as a top solo.

Sources: Lore Fortress, "Morgan Blackhand"; Cyberpunk Wiki (via search results).

## Draft interview

## INTERVIEW WITH THE DEAD: MORGAN BLACKHAND

Here's how it works at the Afterlife. You order a legend's drink and the legend sits down. I asked Claire for a Morgan Blackhand. She said there isn't one. Nobody's sure he's dead.

He sat down anyway.

He came in from about 2023, by the look of it. Big man, plain jacket, the kind of boots that have walked out of more places than they've walked into. One black hand on the bar. He didn't take the stool facing the door. He took the one facing the mirror, so he could see the door and me.

**CT:** Mr. Blackhand. Heard you were dead.

**MB:** People hear a lot of things. You hear the one about me reporting to the President?

**CT:** I did.

**MB:** Good. Keep hearing it.

**CT:** Can I get you anything?

**MB:** Water.

**CT:** At the Afterlife?

**MB:** I drink here when I'm dead. Not before.

**CT:** Let's talk about work. You're the top solo of your time. The rankings say so.

**MB:** Rankings are written by people who sell rankings. I'm the solo who's still employable. That's the only ranking that pays.

**CT:** Who paid you?

**MB:** Mostly Militech. A soldier's a soldier, and a corp's an army that files taxes. I lost the arm for a flag. After that I worked for whoever was honest about being a buyer.

**CT:** Was Militech honest?

**MB:** Militech was on time. That's rarer.

**CT:** 2019. Somebody tried to take Kerry Eurodyne. You took all five of them alive.

**MB:** Dead men don't tell you who hired them.

**CT:** So it wasn't mercy.

**MB:** It wasn't anything. It was the job done right. You want a word for it, use "thorough."

**CT:** Rache Bartmoss.

*(A long pause.)*

**MB:** I was late.

**CT:** That's all?

**MB:** That's the whole report.

**CT:** The roof. 2023. Arasaka Tower. You stayed behind with Adam Smasher so your people could leave.

**MB:** Somebody has to hold the door. Usually it's the guy closest to it.

**CT:** What happened up there?

**MB:** You know what the tower did after.

**CT:** I know what the tower did. I'm asking what you did.

**MB:** I held the door.

**CT:** There's a story going round Japantown. A massacre. The one survivor says the killer was a portly man with a black cyberarm.

**MB:** *(looking at his hand)* Lot of black arms in this city.

**CT:** And portly?

**MB:** Lot of noodles in this city too.

**CT:** Here's the thing, Mr. Blackhand. You're the patron saint of every solo who ever wanted to be a professional, and not a butcher. This zine's for people who'd rather walk out than shoot their way out. You're one of ours.

**MB:** I'm not one of anybody's. That's why I'm alive. *(He stands.)* And kid. The day there's a drink with my name on it, don't order it. Somebody'll be lying.

He left the water. Claire swears nobody came in and nobody went out.
