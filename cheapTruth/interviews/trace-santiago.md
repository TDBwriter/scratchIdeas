# Interview prep: Trace Santiago

**Status by 2077:** whereabouts unknown. Per the Cyberpunk Wiki, his portrait hangs in the Afterlife.
**Canon:** Cyberpunk RED, not Cyberpunk 2020. His father, Nomad Santiago, is the 2020 character.
**Fits the department:** only if it opens to RED characters and to "fate unknown."

## What canon says
- A Media in Cyberpunk RED, and one of the edgerunners in Mike Pondsmith's RED fiction (R. Talsorian Games, June 2020).
- The son of Nomad Santiago, who led the Aldecaldos nomad family in Cyberpunk 2020.
- The character was created with a real collaborator, merged with the existing Santiago family. **That collaborator is a real person and stays out of the interview.**
- A non-canon Cyberpunk V3 made him Nomad Santiago's grandson instead.
- In 2077: his whereabouts are unknown; his portrait is in the Afterlife.
- The RED Media role: a Media's power is Credibility, being believed.

Sources: R. Talsorian Games, "Cyberpunk Red: Meet the new Edgerunners" (June 12, 2020); Cyberpunk Wiki, "Trace Santiago," "Nomad Santiago," "Aldecaldos Members" (via search results).

## Why he matters to the zine
He's a Media, like V-media, and he works under his own name. *Cheap Truth* is anonymous. Trace is the one legend who can argue with Veritas about the craft itself.

## Draft interview

## INTERVIEW WITH THE DEAD: TRACE SANTIAGO

Trace Santiago hangs on the Afterlife wall with the other legends. I don't know if he's dead. I don't think the wall knows either. I ordered two of whatever the bar pours for people who aren't sure, and I looked at the portrait until it looked back.

He came in from the Time of the Red, a few decades back, when the city was still digging itself out. Road dust on the boots, a recorder clipped where some people wear a gun. He looked at my notebook before he looked at me.

**TS:** Who are you with?

**CT:** *Cheap Truth.*

**TS:** And who are you?

**CT:** Veritas.

**TS:** That's not a name. That's a slogan.

**CT:** That's the point.

**TS:** *(sitting)* Then let's start there, because it's the only interesting thing about you. You know what a Media has? One thing. Credibility. People believe you because they know who you are, where you've been, and that you'll still be standing there tomorrow when they come looking. You take your name off it, what are you selling?

**CT:** The story.

**TS:** Everybody's selling the story. The story's free. The name's what costs.

**CT:** Tell me about your name, then.

**TS:** Santiago. My father's name before it was mine. Nomad Santiago, Aldecaldos. A family on the road. You grow up in a convoy, you learn two things early: everybody's job matters, and nobody tells the truth about a road they haven't driven.

**CT:** Is that why you became a Media?

**TS:** I became a Media because somebody had to tell the cities what the roads looked like, and the cities weren't asking. The corps had the screens. The family had the trucks. I had a recorder and a face people could find.

**CT:** Who paid you?

**TS:** Everybody tried. The trick isn't refusing money. The trick is being the guy who can't be bought with it, and letting that be known. That's credibility. You spend it once and it's gone.

**CT:** So what's a Media worth in Night City now?

**TS:** Same as always. Exactly as much as the people who believe him.

**CT:** Here's my case for no name. I name myself, I'm a target. I name my sources, they're bodies. The anonymity is the shield.

**TS:** I know that argument. I've made it, on bad nights. *(He turns the glass.)* It works. For a while. Then somebody prints a lie and signs it "Veritas," and how does anyone tell which Veritas is you?

**CT:** By the work.

**TS:** Maybe. You'd better be very good.

**CT:** You're on the wall here. With the legends. Doesn't that make you one of ours?

**TS:** It makes me a picture. *(He stands, clips the recorder back on.)* You want to be one of mine, kid, put your name on one story. Just one. See how it feels when it's yours.

I didn't. He knew I wouldn't. He tipped two fingers at the portrait on the way out, as if it were somebody he used to know.
