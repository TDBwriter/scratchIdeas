# Interview prep: Rache Bartmoss

**Status by 2077:** dead. Sources disagree on how: an Arasaka team killed him, or his heart stopped in 2020 and a cryo freezer preserved him.
**Fits the department:** yes.

## What canon says
- Born in 1992. Netrunning from age four; a prodigy.
- At CCI Development he wrote the Daemon programs, which "could be used to hack entire networks at a time," and was rich before he was twenty.
- At 17 he met Spider Murphy, his apprentice and best friend.
- He targeted NetWatch and reportedly fried agents' nervous systems.
- In the Combat Zone he forced out tenants without neural implants and controlled the rest of his building with cyberdeck programs.
- In 2014, at 22, his heart stopped for 10 seconds and his machines revived him.
- One account: in 2020, while netrunning, his heart stopped for good. A cryogenic freezer kept his brain, and he carried on as a ghost in the net. In 2021 Militech asked for his help against Arasaka; Alt Cunningham persuaded him, and he uncovered Soulkiller in Arasaka's net.
- The other account: an Arasaka team killed him, and Morgan Blackhand failed to stop it.
- He released the R.A.B.I.D.S. (Roving Autonomous Bartmoss Interface Drones). Within months over 78% of the Net was infected: the DataKrash. NetWatch built the Blackwall afterwards.
- The sourcebook *Rache Bartmoss' Guide to the Net* (1993) is written in his voice.
- In 2077 his body and cyberdeck can be found in his freezer. One source says Revere Courier Services dumped the freezer at the landfill when the storage contract ran out.

Sources: Screen Rant, "Who Rache Bartmoss Really Was" (Nov. 29, 2022); Lore Fortress, "Morgan Blackhand"; Wikipedia, *Rache Bartmoss' Guide to the Net*; search results on the freezer and landfill.

## Draft interview

## INTERVIEW WITH THE DEAD: RACHE BARTMOSS

I didn't order anything for Rache Bartmoss. You don't buy a drink for a man who broke the Net. You leave a port open and wait.

He came in through the bar's payment terminal. Every screen in the Afterlife flickered at once and the ads went quiet, and then there was a thin, pale man on the next stool. He wore clothes that hadn't been washed or changed by anyone, and his eyes moved like he was reading something on the inside of them.

**RB:** Paper.

**CT:** Hello to you too.

**RB:** You printed a zine. In 2077. On paper. In a city where I could put my name on the inside of every eyeball in the room. That's either very stupid or very smart and I haven't decided which.

**CT:** Let me know when you do.

**RB:** You'll know. Your printer will tell you.

**CT:** Let's talk about money. You were rich at nineteen.

**RB:** I was rich at nineteen because CCI was stupid at every age. They paid me to write Daemons. Programs that go through a network like water through sand. They thought they owned them. You don't own water. You own a bucket, briefly.

**CT:** What did you do with the money?

**RB:** Bought machines. Bought privacy. Bought a building.

**CT:** The building in the Combat Zone. You threw out anyone without a neural port.

**RB:** I didn't throw them out. I made it unpleasant for them to stay.

**CT:** That's throwing them out.

**RB:** That's a landlord with a better toolkit than most. You want to cry about it, cry about the landlords who did it with lawyers.

*(This zine doesn't cheer for that. I'm printing it so you know who you're toasting.)*

**CT:** NetWatch.

**RB:** Cops for the net. Who pays the cops? The people who own the places the cops guard. So who are the cops working for?

**CT:** You fried them.

**RB:** I corrected them.

**CT:** And then the R.A.B.I.D.S.

**RB:** Roving Autonomous Bartmoss Interface Drones. Say it right, it's a good acronym.

**CT:** They took down 78% of the Net. You broke the thing everyone in the world was using.

**RB:** Everyone in the world wasn't using it. Six companies were using it, and everyone in the world was paying rent to be inside. I knocked over the building. Did people get hurt? Sure. People always get hurt when you knock over a building that they've been told is their home. Ask who built it with no doors.

**CT:** And now there's a Blackwall.

**RB:** Now there's a wall, and somebody's paid to guard it, and the rent went up. *(Thin smile.)* You see the pattern? I see the pattern. I've always seen the pattern. That's why I'm dead.

**CT:** How did you die?

**RB:** Which time?

**CT:** Any time.

**RB:** My heart stopped at the deck. Or Arasaka came through my door. Pick the one that sells better. I was there for both and I still don't know which version of me is the original.

**CT:** There's a story your freezer ended up at the landfill. The storage contract ran out.

**RB:** *(very still)* Somebody stopped paying.

**CT:** Somebody stopped paying.

**RB:** There it is. The greatest netrunner who ever lived, and in the end I'm an invoice. You want a headline? Put that one. Not the Krash. The invoice.

**CT:** Spider Murphy edited your writing, after.

**RB:** Spider cut the best parts.

**CT:** She says she cut the parts that insulted the reader's mother.

**RB:** Those were the best parts.

**CT:** This zine is a little thing passed hand to hand, outside the corp net. Isn't that what you wanted? You're our grandfather.

**RB:** I don't have grandchildren. I have forks. *(The screens start flickering again.)* You're not outside anything, kid. You're on paper because you're scared. That's fine. Scared is smart. Just don't tell me it's freedom.

Every screen in the bar came back on at once, mid-ad. The stool was empty. My notebook had one new page in it, in handwriting that wasn't mine. All it said was "invoice."
