# Interview prep: Johnny Silverhand, as of Cyberpunk 2020 and RED

**Status by 2077:** dead. Killed in 2023 at Arasaka Tower. The versions differ: in *Firestorm*, Adam Smasher kills him; in 2077, Arasaka soulkills him.
**Called up from:** 2020, before the tower. He knows nothing after that: no Relic, no V, no 2077.
**Fits the department:** yes. He has a drink at the Afterlife.

## What canon says
- First appears in the 1988 Cyberpunk rulebook, in the short story and introductory adventure "Never Fade Away."
- A rockerboy. Frontman of Samurai. By 2013 the band had split and he had a solo career.
- A veteran of the Second Central American Conflict, where the US experimented on its own troops with combat drugs and risky cybernetics.
- His girlfriend Alt Cunningham, a genius programmer, was kidnapped by Arasaka, forced to build Soulkiller, and killed by it.
- The last book of the 2020 line, set in the Fourth Corporate War, has him killed in Arasaka Tower by Adam Smasher, who worked for Saburo Arasaka.
- In Cyberpunk RED he's a dead legend of the Time of the Red.
- In 2077, the Afterlife serves a Johnny Silverhand.

Sources: PC Gamer, "Johnny Silverhand's tragic rock star backstory"; Den of Geek, "Who is Johnny Silverhand?"; Lore Fortress, "Morgan Blackhand." The silver cyberarm is common knowledge across the series and not separately sourced here.

## Draft interview

## INTERVIEW WITH THE DEAD: JOHNNY SILVERHAND

I ordered a Johnny Silverhand. Claire put it down without a word. Half the people who order it are tourists. The other half are about to do something stupid.

He came in from 2020, still alive, which is to say still angry. Shades at night. A silver arm on the bar like he'd set down a guitar case. He drank half the glass before he looked at what was in it.

**JS:** Who made this?

**CT:** The bar. It's named after you.

**JS:** *(laughs)* They name drinks after dead guys.

**CT:** I know.

**JS:** *(a beat)* Yeah. Figured.

**CT:** Let's start with the band.

**JS:** Samurai. Best thing I ever did and the thing I'll never get out from under. You put a band together because you've got something to say. Then a label buys it and you've got something to sell. Then you've got a contract that says how loud you're allowed to say it.

**CT:** Who got paid?

**JS:** Everybody but the people in the front row. The label. The arenas. The corps that sponsored the arenas. You know who never got paid? The kids who learned the words. They paid us.

**CT:** You went solo.

**JS:** I went honest. Smaller rooms, louder songs.

**CT:** Before the music, the war.

**JS:** *(he flexes the silver hand)* Central America. Our own government ran us like lab rats. Combat drugs, chrome that hadn't been tested on anybody who could sue. They sent kids down there to find out what kills kids. I came back with this and a list.

**CT:** A list?

**JS:** Of people who owe. Most of them run corps now.

**CT:** Alt.

*(He sets the glass down.)*

**JS:** You don't get to say her name like it's a track on an album.

**CT:** Then tell me.

**JS:** Alt Cunningham was the smartest person in any room she walked into, including the ones with me in them. Arasaka took her because she was the best. They made her build the thing that killed her. Then they called it a product.

**CT:** Follow the money on that.

**JS:** You don't need to follow it. It's got a logo on the side.

**CT:** People in this city still wear your face. On shirts. On walls.

**JS:** Who's selling the shirts?

**CT:** Somebody always is.

**JS:** Then that somebody's my enemy too. *(He leans in.)* You want to know what I am? I'm not a saint. I'm not the city's conscience. I'm a guy with a guitar and a grudge, and the grudge is bigger.

**CT:** This zine thinks you're one of us. Truth to power.

**JS:** You print on paper and don't sign your name. I played stadiums with my face forty feet high. We are not the same, choom. *(He finishes the drink.)* But, hey. Keep at it. Somebody's got to be the opening act.

He left the glass upside down on the bar. I didn't tell him about 2023. Nobody tells you about your last show.
