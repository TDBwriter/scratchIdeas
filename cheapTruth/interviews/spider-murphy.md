# Interview prep: Spider Murphy

**Status by 2077:** not found. One fan discussion says she survives the 2023 raid; nothing confirms her status in RED or 2077.
**Fits the department:** only if "believed dead" is stretched to "fate unknown." See the open questions in `../INTERVIEWS_WITH_THE_DEAD.md`.

## What canon says
- A Cyberpunk 2020 netrunner.
- She met Rache Bartmoss in her teens (he was 17) and became his apprentice, partner in crime and best friend.
- His style was flamboyant and aggressive. **Hers favored utility and non-lethal attacks.**
- She was part of the 2023 attack on Arasaka Tower. Her focus was destroying Arasaka's upgraded Soulkiller and setting Alt Cunningham free.
- She told Alt about Bartmoss's death. Alt gave her a piece of Soulkiller to use if needed.
- In *Firestorm: Shockwave* (an alternate timeline, per the wiki), she recovered Alt's personality file and threw it somewhere on the net to be retrieved later.
- She edited Bartmoss's writings after his death.
- In *CyberGeneration*, Bartmoss recruits players to find and rescue her.

Sources: Cyberpunk Wiki, "Spider Murphy" and "Altiera Cunningham" (via search results); Screen Rant, "Who Rache Bartmoss Really Was"; World Anvil (fan source, low weight).

## Draft interview

## INTERVIEW WITH THE DEAD: SPIDER MURPHY

The Afterlife has no drink for Spider Murphy. I ordered a coffee and left the chair beside me empty. Netrunners don't come in through the door.

She came in through the jukebox. The screen went white, then gray, then a woman in a worn jacket was sitting on the stool, plugs at her temple, looking at the room the way a runner looks at a building: where the wires go.

**CT:** Spider Murphy.

**SM:** Depends who's asking. Who's asking?

**CT:** A zine.

**SM:** Paper. *(She almost smiles.)* Rache would've hated you.

**CT:** He hated everybody.

**SM:** He hated everybody out loud. It's different. You learn more from someone who says it.

**CT:** You were his apprentice.

**SM:** I was his partner. Apprentice is what you call it when you want the man to get the credit.

**CT:** How did you meet?

**SM:** I was a kid. He was a kid with money. CCI paid him like a god for writing Daemons, and he spent it like a teenager, which he was. He showed me how to get into anything. I showed him you didn't have to burn it down when you left.

**CT:** That's the part I came for. Your runs were non-lethal.

**SM:** My runs were efficient.

**CT:** You didn't fry people.

**SM:** Frying people is loud. Loud brings NetWatch. Loud brings the corp's ice. Loud brings their friends next week. You leave a man alive and confused, he spends the next day explaining to his boss what happened, and nobody believes him. That's a better outcome for me than a body.

**CT:** So it's tactics.

**SM:** It's both. I won't pretend it isn't both. But if I tell you it's mercy, you'll put me on a poster.

**CT:** 2023. Arasaka Tower.

**SM:** Alt.

**CT:** You went in for Alt Cunningham.

**SM:** We went in to break Soulkiller before Arasaka made it worse. Alt was inside it. You don't get one without the other.

**CT:** You were the one who told her about Rache.

*(She looks at the jukebox for a while.)*

**SM:** Somebody had to. Better me than a log file.

**CT:** And she gave you something.

**SM:** She gave me a piece of the thing that killed her. Told me to use it if I needed to. That's the worst present anyone ever gave me, and I've been given a deck by Rache Bartmoss.

**CT:** Did you need to?

**SM:** Ask me when you've got clearance.

**CT:** After he died, you edited his writing.

**SM:** Somebody had to cut out the parts where he insulted the reader's mother. It was most of the parts.

**CT:** Here's what I want to say. This zine is for people who go in, get the truth, and leave nobody dead behind them. You're who we'd be if we were good at it.

**SM:** No. *(She stands. The jukebox flickers.)* You'd be you. I was a runner with a style, and the style kept me alive. Don't make a saint out of a skill set. Saints get martyred. Runners get paid.

**CT:** Are you dead, Ms. Murphy?

**SM:** Dead's a status flag. I set my own.

The jukebox played the first two seconds of a song nobody had picked. Then the screen went back to the menu.
