MORE TROUBLE

SMOF-BBS, "the world's first on-line SF fanzine,"
wires on January 26, 1985, Operating on T-Net Version 2.0
telecommunications software, SMOF-BBS is up around the clock and is
accessible at 300 baud through modems anywhere on the planet. Plug
in and call 512-UFO-SMOF.

SERVING SF THRU SAMIZD ATA

CHEAP TRUTH issues 1-10 have been placed on special files
within the system. And, as always, CHEAP TRUTH is Not Copyrighted.
It can now be read from the screen, or downloaded and printed out at
home. And for the first time, CHEAP TRUTH has a letters column.

Excerpts from letters received at CHEAP TRUTH CENTRAL will
henceforth be entered into Section 3 of the system. There they'll
merge with comments typed in over Phone lines from remote locations,
This electronic lettercol will be periodically printed out, xeroxed,
and mailed to CHEAP TRUTH conspirators. Together we will destroy
the artificial boundary between wire and print.

USE IT--OR BE USED BY UIT

From INTERZONE 8: "Some would argue that the new electronic
gadgetry is displacing the printed word -- if 80, writers should
fight back, using guerilla tactics as necessary and infiltrating the
territory of the enemy." We ask devotees of print everywhere to
battle alongside us in our guerilla invasion. Computer bulletin
boards are a prose medium, requiring the same talent, vision, and
dedication that prose requires in magazines, newspapers, books. The

bottleneck, as always, is in imagination. Help us make the hardware
worthwhile.

Those of you with word processors are aware of the sudden
leaping power that computers can give. In SMOF-BBS, we hope to give
a similar quantum leap in ease and distribution -~ to the fanzine.

We have boards open for criticism, comment, and exchange of
data, on topics ranging throughout the SF spectrum, from comics and
video through Japanese animation and simulations gaming,

We hope to make SMOF-BBS a library, a fanzine, a databank,
and an on-going convention by wire -- all Simultaneously. We have
24 megabytes of empty storage -- 300,000 words of potential. Help
us fulfill it.

CHEAP TRUTH will continue to Operate in parallel through our
established underground network of xerox pirates and the
international mails. If you received this notice, you are on our
Interactive Videofiles and will receive future issues without
warning. Call SMOF-BBS for back issues or request printouts from
your local cyberhead. And write our new Lettercolumn -- preferably
in chunks of a hundred 80-column lines or less.

We have received many letters in the past, and we thank all
those concerned. From now on, comments sent us may be offered free
of copyright to anyone with a phone and a modem. Get ready.
