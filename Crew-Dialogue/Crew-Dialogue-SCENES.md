# Crew Dialogue: the scenes

The storyline's scenes, in order. Each lists when it happens, where, who's in frame, the shot, the dialogue, and which arcs it moves. Locations marked "pick in game" still need scouting. The rules are in `Crew-Dialogue.md`; each character's behavior and voice are in that character's file.

### 1. Lunch with a stranger
- **When:** The Streetkid, right after they wake. [GAME: they decide to get lunch]
- **Where:** a food stall in Heywood (pick in game).
- **In frame:** V-media and Jackie.
- **The shot:** two bruised faces over two bowls, the camera on the counter between them.

> **JACKIE:** About the gun. Nothin' personal, compa. Just biz. [GAME line] I had a guy, you had a guy. Our guys had the same idea.
> **V:** Your guy get you out of the cuffs?
> **JACKIE:** Some cop got us out of the cuffs. With his fist.
> **JACKIE:** So what was in it for you? You don't look like you boost cars for fun.
> **V:** Bartender owes money. Due tomorrow.
> **JACKIE:** And you're his what? Family?
> **V:** I drink there.
> **JACKIE:** You stole a car for the guy who pours your drinks.
> **V:** Tried to.
> **JACKIE:** Hermano. That's the dumbest or the best thing I heard all year.
> *(He nods at the camera.)*
> **JACKIE:** And that? You shoot people with it?
> **V:** Just pictures.
> **JACKIE:** You don't carry?
> **V:** Don't need to.
> **JACKIE:** Everybody in this city needs to.
> **V:** Then somebody should be watching them do it.
> **JACKIE:** *(a beat)* Jackie Welles.
> **V:** V.
> **JACKIE:** Okay, V. Next job I get, you're on it. You watch. I'll carry.

**Moves:** the crew strikes its deal; V-media names the refusal; Jackie picks a partner by heart, not sense.

### 2. Sauce on his chin
- **When:** the six months (the montage).
- **Where:** a rooftop in Watson (pick in game).
- **In frame:** Jackie. V-media behind the camera.
- **The shot:** Jackie against the skyline, eating, not posing.

> **JACKIE:** You gonna take a picture or eat?
> **V:** Both. Hold still.
> **JACKIE:** Make me look like a legend.
> **V:** You look like a guy with sauce on his chin.
> **JACKIE:** Then that's the legend. Legends are born here, mano. [He says it again in the game, in The Rescue.] *(He wipes it off and poses.)* Now?
> **V:** The first one was better.

**Moves:** V-media's first portrait of Jackie; the legend Jackie wants against the man he is. Scene 12 calls back to it.

### 3. She picked us

- **Where:** wherever Practice Makes Perfect starts (check in game).
- **In frame:** V-media and Jackie. T-Bug on the call.

> **JACKIE:** Got you a present. Militech training shard. Real deal.
> **T-BUG:** *(cutting in)* Man, Militech can't encrypt for shit. [GAME line] I've been inside it since breakfast.
> **JACKIE:** T-Bug! Say hi to V.
> **T-BUG:** Hi, V. Jackie talks about you too much.
> **V:** You're the netrunner.
> **T-BUG:** I'm the one who checks. Jackie vouches for everyone. I don't.
> **JACKIE:** She's picky.
> **T-BUG:** I'm alive. Same thing. Slot it. Hope you're ready to bust ass through this fascist playground. [GAME line]

**Moves:** T-Bug's Call (an audition); the crew's Mentor arrives; three of them together for the first time.

### 4. Three minutes out
- **When:** the drive home after The Rescue. [GAME: a police cordon; V talks the officer into letting them through]
- **Where:** the car, at the cordon.
- **In frame:** V-media and Jackie. T-Bug on the call.
- **The shot:** the cordon lights through the windshield.

> **T-BUG:** Trauma Team's got her. Platinum client, platinum service.
> **JACKIE:** And the lady in the first room?
> **T-BUG:** Wasn't a client.
> *(Silence.)*
> **V:** I took her picture.
> **JACKIE:** The dead one? Why?
> **V:** Somebody should know she was there.
> **T-BUG:** Nobody pays to know that, V.
> **V:** I know.
> **T-BUG:** *(a beat)* Send it to me. For the file. I keep files. Cuttin' my wires now. [GAME line]

**Moves:** V-media's witness theme starts; T-Bug's first crack (she keeps it); Jackie learns why V watches.

### 5. Eat, then eyes
- **When:** the start of The Ripperdoc. [GAME: Jackie calls from the food stall downstairs; V drives them to Viktor's]
- **Where:** the food stall below V's building.
- **In frame:** Jackie eating; V-media arriving.
- **The shot:** Jackie mid-bite under the building's lights.

> **JACKIE:** Sit, choomie. Eat. You don't walk into Vik's on an empty stomach.
> **V:** What's he putting in?
> **JACKIE:** Kiroshis. Best eyes in Watson for the price.
> **V:** I've got a camera.
> **JACKIE:** Now you'll have two. And this one you can't drop off a roof.
> **V:** How much?
> **JACKIE:** Twenty-one large.
> **V:** Jackie.
> **JACKIE:** Pay when the job pays. Vik's good for it. So are you.

**Moves:** Jackie as V-media's mentor; the lens becomes part of V. It sets up the game's choice to pay Viktor now or later.

### 6. Every camera's a window
- **When:** The Gift. [GAME: T-Bug sends V to Yoko for the PING quickhack, to try on a camera]
- **Where:** Kabuki Market, at the camera.
- **In frame:** V-media at the camera. T-Bug on the call.

> **T-BUG:** You got it from Yoko?
> **V:** In my hand.
> **T-BUG:** Slot it and point yourself at the camera. I'll do the rest.
> *(Click. V's photo of the camera fills the frame.)*
> **T-BUG:** Pinged. Now I see everything it's wired to. Every camera on that net just lit up.
> **V:** That's all it does?
> **T-BUG:** That's all I need it to do. I've got maybe a dozen tricks, V. I didn't write one of them. I just never miss with the ones I've got.
> **V:** Cameras are windows.
> **T-BUG:** Most of them face in. Remember that when you're standing in front of one.
> **V:** Who's watching me through them?
> **T-BUG:** Today? Me.

**In the edit:** the player's PING is covered by V's photo of the camera. The hack is T-Bug's. This is the first use of the photo cover.

**Moves:** T-Bug shows V-media her trade and admits to a fixed bag of tricks; the witness theme turns to surveillance, which is who watches whom.

### 7. Noodles, and a third voice
- **When:** free roam, after The Gift.
- **Where:** a noodle stall in Kabuki Market (pick in game).
- **In frame:** V-media and Jackie on stools. T-Bug on a three-way call, shown on the phone screen.
- **The shot:** two backs at the counter, steam, the phone face-up between the bowls with T-Bug's call on it.

> *(The call chimes. Neither of them called.)*
> **JACKIE:** Did you...
> **V:** No.
> **T-BUG:** You two chew like you're paid by the slurp.
> **JACKIE:** Bug! Where are you?
> **T-BUG:** Somewhere with better food. *(a beat)* That's a lie. It's protein paste.
> **V:** Come down. Kabuki. We'll save you a stool.
> **T-BUG:** I don't do stools. Stools are how they find you.
> **JACKIE:** Who's they?
> **T-BUG:** Whoever's paying them this week.
> **V:** You called us, though.
> **T-BUG:** I pinged the market this morning. The stall's camera was on the list. You two walked into frame.
> **V:** For a job? Or to check on us?
> *(A pause.)*
> **T-BUG:** The broth's too salty. Get the number four.
> **JACKIE:** *(to the cook)* Number four! *(to the phone)* So what's your number, Bug? For retirement. How much?
> **T-BUG:** More than this city wants to pay.
> **JACKIE:** And where would you go?
> **T-BUG:** Somewhere with no signal.
> **V:** You'd go crazy in a week.
> **T-BUG:** That's the plan. Go crazy slow. On a porch.
> **JACKIE:** Me, I want a seat at the Afterlife. My name on the wall. A drink with my name on it.
> **T-BUG:** You want them to remember you. I want them to forget me. One of us is going to be disappointed.
> *(V raises the camera at the phone screen.)*
> **V:** Smile.
> **T-BUG:** Point that at me and I'll brick it.
> *(Click.)*
> **T-BUG:** ...Send me that one.

**Moves:** the first time T-Bug calls without a job; Jackie says his want out loud; V-media's camera reaches her. Jackie's line pays off: since Update 1.5 the Afterlife serves a Jackie Welles (vodka, lime juice, ginger beer, "a splash of love"), so after The Heist V can order one.

### 8. The way up
- **When:** free roam.
- **Where:** a climbable rooftop in Watson (pick in game).
- **In frame:** V-media at the roof's edge; Jackie small in the street below. T-Bug on the call.
- **The shot:** V at the edge with the city behind, Jackie looking up.

> **JACKIE:** How'd you get up there?
> **V:** Pipe, sign, AC unit, other sign.
> **JACKIE:** That's not a way up. That's a way down with extra steps.
> **T-BUG:** I've got the corner camera. All fine. Jackie, you're blocking a delivery van.
> **JACKIE:** Whose side are you on?
> **T-BUG:** The side with the view. V, go left. Behind the billboard there's a ladder nobody's touched in years.
> **V:** How do you know?
> **T-BUG:** I've been up there. *(a beat)* Through the camera.
> **V:** Not the same.
> **T-BUG:** ...No. It's not.

**Moves:** V-media shows T-Bug what she's missing; Jackie learns V's world; the crew's roles work together, with T-Bug as spotter.

### 9. The gig of a lifetime
- **When:** after Dex's pitch in The Ride. [GAME: V steps out, takes calls, briefs Jackie] [OURS: T-Bug cuts in]

> **JACKIE:** That's why it's the one.
> **T-BUG:** *(cutting in)* I don't do Arasaka.
> **JACKIE:** Bug...
> **T-BUG:** I don't. Do. Arasaka. Ugh, hate this life or death shit. [GAME line] *(a beat)* What's the cut?
> **V:** Enough for a porch?
> *(Silence.)*
> **T-BUG:** Enough for a porch and a fence.
> **JACKIE:** So that's a yes.
> **T-BUG:** That's a "send me the shard."
> **V:** Nobody gets hurt. That's my cut.

**Moves:** each says yes for their own reason: Jackie for the legend, T-Bug for the porch, V-media for them. Dex asking for it "as clean as possible" is [GAME].
