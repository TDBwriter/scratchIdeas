# Crew Dialogue: T-Bug

Everything needed to keep T-Bug's behavior and voice right. What the game shows of her is extremely important to keep. We can play with it, but not change what she is.

---

## In the game

**The facts**
- **Real name:** none given in the sources found.
- **Voice:** Cynthia Kaye McWilliams, per IMDb's credits. That page couldn't be opened directly to confirm.
- **Class:** Netrunner. One wiki lists her location as Westbrook, with no specific place.
- **Background, from the wiki:**
  - "a textbook example of how solos and netrunners really aren't all that different," and she is "well-aware of this fact."
  - A specialist who can be picky about her contracts.
  - Unlike more reckless netrunners, she "intends to sail into retirement age."
- **Role:** V and Jackie work with her now and then on small gigs before Dex's job.
- **Death:** killed in the net during the Konpeki Plaza lockdown. Sources differ on how it's described: Arasaka's netrunners kill her in the security sweep, or the Arasaka subnet detects her and fries her brain remotely. The game moves past it quickly; one commentary notes the player only learns she's dead when V mentions it later.

**T-Bug and the crew on screen, before The Heist**

**What she does in the game (her skills)**
- **Practice Makes Perfect:** rewrites a Militech training shard and runs V through shooting, hacking, stealth and combat scenarios.
- **The Rescue:**
  - Briefs the crew over comms and marks a route through the scavenger den.
  - Tells V to pull a shard from Sandra's biomonitor, which lets its call to Trauma Team get through.
- **The Gift:** sends V to get the PING quickhack and try it on a camera.
- **The Information:** joins a braindance through V's link.
- **The Heist:**
  - At check-in, tells V to stop the receptionist before she calls Taki-san.
  - The Flathead goes through the vents to plug her into the hotel's systems. Once she's in, she links V to the CCTV.
  - Tells V to escape through the window.

**Her limits in the game**
- **She works remotely,** through V and through devices on site. To get into Arasaka's systems she needed a physical way in: the Flathead and the netrunner chair.
- **She directs, and the player does the hands-on hacking.**
- **She's in the room once:** Dex's briefing at the Afterlife.
- **She dies in the net.**

**After The Heist:** no messages, memorial or job about her turned up in the sources found. Left blank.

## Behavior

**Canon to keep**
- **The line:** [GAME] is what the game requires. [OURS] is what the playthrough makes appear to be true. The two are only at odds if that line is forgotten.
- **Comms only:** T-Bug is on remote comms in every scene where the game doesn't put her in the room.
- **On screen, the hacks are hers.** In the game, the player performs every hack. In the edit, each hack is covered by a still photo that V appears to have snapped, laid over the footage the way the 1960s Batman show covered its missed punches with a card. The story credits the hack to T-Bug.
- **A fixed bag of tricks.** In this telling T-Bug is a script kiddie: she can only repeat the tricks she has. She is only ever shown doing a hack that is seen in gameplay. (The game's own description calls her a specialist who is picky about contracts. The bag of tricks is ours.)
- **Play, don't change:** we can play with her canon (what she eats, her porch, her jokes) but not change what she is.

## Voice

- **Dry. Short sentences.** Often a one-word correction.
- **Numbers, cameras, odds.**
- **Deflects with retirement jokes:** the porch, no signal.
- **Says the true thing sideways, then changes the subject:** "The broth's too salty. Get the number four."
- **Calls them "V" and "Jackie."**
- **From the game:** "Relax, Judy. Everything'll be fine."

**Lines that sound like T-Bug**
- "I'm the one who checks. Jackie vouches for everyone. I don't."
- "I'm alive. Same thing."
- "I don't do stools. Stools are how they find you."
- "I've got maybe a dozen tricks, V. I didn't write one of them. I just never miss with the ones I've got."
- "I don't. Do. Arasaka. What's the cut?"
- "...Send me that one."

## With the others

- **Jackie:** he vouches, she audits. She worries about him through his mother: "Pick up tonight. Before the big one."
- **V:** she shows V her trade. V keeps asking her into the room, and V's camera is the one thing that gets a "send me that" out of her.
