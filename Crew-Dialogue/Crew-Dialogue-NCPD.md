# Crew Dialogue: the road to Regina

How the NCPD scanner work leads the crew to Regina Jones. The NCPD jobs give little subject of their own, so they carry this one arc between them.

---

## What the game gives

**The 14 entries before The Heist**

| Kind | Jobs | The shape of each |
|---|---|---|
| Reported crimes (6) | Paranoia, Dangerous Currents, Protect and Serve, One Thing Led to Another, Needle in a Haystack, Don't Forget the Parking Brake | A scene, then a trail. Something left behind (a shard, a computer's messages, a corpse) points to a hidden stash somewhere else. |
| Organized crime (5) | Rooftops by Drake Ave, Market by Goldsmith St, The Clinic, The Camp, The Plant | A gang holding a place, and a container. |
| Assaults in progress (3 entries, 24 sites) | Little China, Kabuki, Northside | A fight, and a bag nearby. |

**The trails, as far as the guides describe them**

| Job | Who's there | What points the way | Where the stash is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paranoia | Two scavengers | A shard on a body | A rooftop in north Little China |
| Dangerous Currents | Maelstrom, around a container | A shard in the container | A crate underwater |
| Protect and Serve | Six Tyger Claws on Kabuki rooftops | Messages on a computer | A container on a higher roof, named "Cops' Cash" |
| One Thing Led to Another | Tyger Claws and a drone | A computer gives an antenna's location | Up a tower |
| Needle in a Haystack | Three Tyger Claws and a body | Details on the corpse | A container nearby |
| Don't Forget the Parking Brake | Scavengers | Messages on a computer about a sunken van | Underwater, by the van |

**What the shards and messages actually say is not in the guides.** Each one is a small story somebody left behind. They have to be read in the game, and the arc below should bend to fit them.

**The link to Regina that's already true:** her gigs open as V's Street Cred rises, and scanner work is a way to earn it. So in the game, doing the NCPD's jobs really is what brings Regina's calls.

---

## Our arc

Five beats, spread across the NCPD jobs. Protect and Serve is always the last job, and its stash is the final reveal. Everything here is [OURS] unless marked.

### 1. The scanner
The crew starts taking NCPD scanner calls because they pay and nobody asks who you are.
- **V:** these are practice. A scene, some light, nobody posing.
- **Jackie:** it's cop work, and he says so. Then he's the first one out of the car.
- **T-Bug:** she likes the scanner because it's a list of where not to be. "It's a map of bad ideas. I just read it backwards."

### 2. The dead leave directions
The reported crimes. Somebody hid something, and left a note about where.
- **V** stops shooting the fight and starts shooting the note: the shard, the screen, the body. This is V's first picture story, a sequence with a beginning and an end.
- **Jackie** hears every note as a will: somebody's last word about who gets what.
- **T-Bug** refuses to read them aloud, then corrects Jackie when he gets one wrong.

### 3. Somebody else is reading
Someone has noticed who keeps closing Watson's scanner calls without leaving bodies.
- A call comes from a number none of them has. [GAME line] "Hey, V. Regina Jones here. If you're looking for work in Watson, give me a call."
- When V calls, she describes their last three scenes back to them, including what V stopped to photograph. [GAME line] "I know where to gather my intel. Could even call me a collector."
- **T-Bug** goes silent. Nobody is supposed to know their route.

### 4. The assignment
- **Regina:** "You don't clear a scene. You read it."
- She offers work: gigs with a subject, and people she wants alive. [GAME line] "But you're not there to execute anybody."
- **Jackie** hears a fixer saying their names.
- **T-Bug** hears a stranger who found them.
- **V** hears an editor.
- The scanner jobs go on between her gigs.

### 5. Cops' Cash: the final reveal
[GAME] On a Kabuki roof, the last trail ends at a stash named "Cops' Cash."

**What it shows:** the cycle of money and violence that corporate rule keeps turning. The department that pays the crew to clear a gang off a roof has money waiting on that same roof. The scanner pays for the violence, the violence protects the payments, and the money comes back round. Nobody on the street set it up, and everybody on the street keeps it going. The crew has been part of it since their first call.

- **V:** the first truth too dangerous to sign. It's the first time V understands what a picture can cost the people in it.
- **Jackie:** the ladder is the wheel. The big league he's climbing toward pays the same people.
- **T-Bug:** the money finds everybody. She was right about that, and wishes she hadn't been.
- **Regina's goodbye, the next time they speak, is the game's:** [GAME line] "Remember, the media lies."
- **The crew:** they go on doing the work anyway. Nobody has to say it.

**Why it matters for The Heist:** this is the crew's last lesson before Dex. They know the game is rigged, and they take the big job anyway, each for their own reason.
