# Crew Dialogue: the supporting cast

The people around the crew before The Heist.

**The rule.** Everyone who works with V before The Heist is in scope. They don't get equal time. Each is there to help the crew's development: to give one of the three something to push against, learn from or lose. A character gets a file of their own once they're developed. So far: Regina (`Crew-Dialogue-REGINA.md`).
