# Crew Dialogue: Regina Jones

A supporting character. Everything needed to keep her behavior and voice right when she's on the line with the crew.

---

## In the game

- **Who:** the fixer for Watson, based in Kabuki. Of Chinese heritage.
- **Before:** an independent reporter for FTF Radio. When News 54 bought the station, she went looking for "a better medium to spread her message."
- **The game's line on her:** "always been a fighter, a girl from the streets." And: "before she bought gossip and sold news - now she spins both."
- **Her contacts** run "from the highest-ranking corpos to the lowliest dealers, gangers, mercs, bums and junkies."
- **What she hands out:**
  - Watson's gigs. All 22 before The Heist are hers.
  - The cyberpsycho sightings, 17 across the city and 6 in Watson. She wants them stopped alive. One guide says she wants the data on why each one snapped, and is trying to understand and cure cyberpsychosis.
- **How she reaches V:**
  - During The Ride she calls to say cyberpsycho attacks are rising.
  - After that she calls whenever V is near a sighting or a gig, and calls again to close each job.
  - V texts her the evidence found at each cyberpsycho scene.
  - Once every sighting is done, V meets her in Kabuki.
- **Who she's tied to:** Max, a reporter she mentored, in the gig Freedom of the Press.
- **Her gigs open with Street Cred.** NCPD scanner work is one of the ways V earns it.

Not confirmed: one low-quality source says she was once a NetWatch agent. It conflicts with the reporter background and is left out.

Sources: Fextralife, "Regina Jones"; Playnews, "Regina's 17 Cyberpsychosis Sightings"; TV Tropes (via search results); the pre-Heist planning log.

---

## In our storyline

- **What she is:** the crew's assignment editor. She never stopped being a reporter. She files her stories with mercs now.
- **Wants:** the truth about why people in her district break, and somebody to go and get it. [Built on the GAME: she wants the cyberpsychos alive]
- **Under the surface:** she lost her station to a corporation and has never forgiven herself for staying alive by spinning. [OURS]
- **How she finds the crew:** through their NCPD scanner work (`Crew-Dialogue-NCPD.md`).
- **Her job in a gig:** she names the subject. Her brief is the assignment, and the four moments in `Crew-Dialogue-GIGS.md` hang on it.

---

## Behavior

- **Calls; never visits.** In the game she is a voice and a face on a call until the very end.
- **Assigns like an editor:** who, what, where, and why it matters to Watson.
- **Wants people alive.** A corpse can't tell her anything.
- **Pays on delivery, and says exactly what for.**
- **Already knows.** By the time she calls, she has the outline. She wants the part she couldn't get from a desk.
- **Protects her sources,** including the crew.
- **Never asks T-Bug's name.**

## Voice

- **Brisk. An editor's voice:** cuts to the lead, cuts the adjectives.
- **Questions that are instructions:** "Who else has this?" "What's your second source?"
- **Dry praise, rare:** "That'll run."
- **Street under the polish.** She was a kid from these blocks before she was a reporter.

**What they show:** she opens with her name and the offer, calls herself a collector of intel, and signs off with a warning about her old trade.

## With the crew

- **V:** she sees herself, twenty years back, and it frightens her a little. She is V's mentor and V's warning: this is what happens to a reporter when somebody buys the outlet. She keeps testing one thing: will V sell the picture, or tell it?
- **Jackie:** a Watson fixer's notice is a rung on his ladder, and he's on his best behavior. She likes him at once, and tells him he's too honest for the big league.
- **T-Bug:** Regina is the one who found them, and T-Bug hates being watched by a professional. They trade insults like colleagues. Regina never asks her name, and T-Bug notices.

## Never

- Never in the room with the crew before The Heist.
- Never lies to them about a job. She leaves things out.
- Never asks for a body when she could have a witness.
