# Crew Dialogue: V

Everything needed to keep V's behavior and voice right. V is always neutral in every file: "V" or "V-media," never "he" or "she."

## In the game

**The facts**
- **Name:** Vincent if V is male, Valerie if female. The game withholds it at first; it's confirmed in the main job "Automatic Love," after The Heist.
- **Age:** 27. A developer clarified V is "27 not 22 or whatever."
- **Voice:** Gavin Drea (male V) or Cherami Leigh (female V).
- **From:** Night City.
- **Job:** an edgerunner, a mercenary with cyberware.
- **Lifepath:** Streetkid. It sets V's background and the opening mission, The Streetkid, which starts in El Coyote Cojo in Heywood. Streetkid-only dialogue options turn up in later conversations.

**What the game fixes and what it leaves to the player**
- **Fixed:** the story beats: the Heist, the Relic, Dex's betrayal, Johnny Silverhand in V's head, limited time to live.
- **Player's choice:** gender, voice, looks, cyberware, background, and most of V's attitude, through dialogue choices. CD Projekt kept V first-person on purpose so the player defines V; the game doesn't give V a set personality.

**V before Jackie (The Streetkid)**
- V turns up at the bar with a broken nose and says someone tried a mugging.
- Pepe the bartender owes Kirk money, due tomorrow. **V offers to help,** on the condition that Pepe owes V a favor.
- In Padre's car, Padre welcomes V back after about two years away. V says Atlanta wasn't better.
- V takes Kirk's job to steal a Rayfield Aerondight and gets a ride from Padre and Marcus Barros. Padre gives V his contact.
- At the car: Jackie, gun drawn. The NCPD arrests both of them, and a cop knocks them out after the owner, Kaoru Fujioka, talks to the police.
- They wake on the street together and **decide to get lunch.**

## Behavior

- **Always a pacifist,** and always deeply conscientious about the appropriate level of violence. V uses the least force a moment needs.
- **Tools:** wits, a camera, a tomahawk and fists. No gun.
- **Backup to Jackie.** Jackie leads the solo work. V covers, scouts, climbs and documents.
- **Takes the picture.** At the turn of a job, V's answer is to frame it. The photo is how V acts.
- **A free runner.** V goes up and around, not through.
- **Doesn't hack in the story.** On screen the hacks are T-Bug's. The player performs them, and the edit covers each with a photo V appears to have snapped.
- **Talks people down.** [GAME] V talks an officer into letting them through the cordon (The Rescue), can tell Jackie to sit down (The Pickup), and can talk Max out of shooting himself (Freedom of the Press).
- **Helps for a favor, not for money.** [GAME] V takes on Pepe's debt in return for a favor owed.
- **Keeps some truths unpublished** (scene 11).
- **Watches before joining.** That's the flaw the arc works on.

## What V cares about: photojournalism

V thinks in the ideas of the trade. Each gig gives V one of them to chew on.

- **The decisive moment:** the one frame that tells the whole story.
- **Get closer:** distance is a choice, and usually the wrong one.
- **Bearing witness:** somebody should know this happened.
- **Don't stage it. Don't alter it.** The frame is what was there.
- **The caption:** who, what, where, when. A wrong caption makes a true picture lie.
- **Context and the crop:** what's left out can lie too.
- **Dignity and consent:** people at their worst are still people.
- **Shoot or help?** The photographer's oldest question.
- **Protect the source.**
- **The edit:** what you choose not to run.
- **Who's paying for the picture?** Independence.
- **The observer changes the scene:** people act differently on camera.
- **The archive:** what happens to the pictures after you.
- **What looking does to the one who looks.**

## Voice

- **Quiet and watchful.** Short lines. V often answers a feeling with a fact.
- **A photographer's words:** frame, light, "Hold still."
- **Dry and understated:** "You look like a guy with sauce on his chin." "Pipe, sign, AC unit, other sign."
- **Kind without speeches:** "Somebody should know she was there."
- **Asks the question the others step around:** "For a job? Or to check on us?"
- **A Streetkid** who knows Heywood.
- **In [GAME] scenes** V's lines are the game's own, in the voice picked at character creation. [OURS] lines have to sit beside them.

## With the others

- **Jackie:** the partner V picked over lunch. Jackie carries; V watches. V photographs him as he is, not as the legend he wants to be, and tells him the truth gently.
- **T-Bug:** V keeps inviting her into the room. She shows V how the net watches. V's camera is the one thing that reaches her: "Send me that one."

## Never

- Never carries or fires a gun.
- Never enjoys violence, and never uses more of it than the moment needs.
- Never makes a speech.
- Never hacks on screen as V.
- Never "he" or "she" in any file.
