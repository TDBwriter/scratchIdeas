# Crew Dialogue: The Streetkid, told late

**Adopted.** The game makes you play The Streetkid first. The videos tell it later, after all the side gigs that look as if the crew ran them together. It becomes the crew's first job across the water, and the start of the big leagues.

---

## What the footage says, and what that costs

The game plays The Streetkid first, and its footage is fixed. Read as a later job, some of it helps and some of it has to go.

**What helps**
- **Heywood is across the water** from Watson, and the job really is the crew's first one out of the district.
- **Padre, Heywood's fixer, gives V a ride, says they could work together again, and hands over his number.** That is the door to the big leagues, already in the game.
- **Padre welcomes V back after about two years away.** Cut one detail (below) and it reads as two years since V worked Heywood.
- **Jackie says he knows V "from the Coyote,"** and that Kirk doesn't mess with people in Heywood.
- **Jackie's own lines fit a friend better than a stranger:** "Nothin' personal, compa. Just biz." and "You help my homies, you're OK in my book. No harm, no foul."
- **The owner, Kaoru Fujioka, calls them street trash and tells the officer to break their legs and dump them in the sea.** After "Cops' Cash," that lands as proof.

**What has to be cut in the edit**
1. **Atlanta.** Padre asks how things went "in the East," and V says Atlanta wasn't better. Those lines say V has been out of Night City.
2. **The introductions.** After they wake, V and Jackie give each other their names for the first time.
3. **The game's narration** that the two then become friends and start working together.

**What can't be cut, only lived with**
- **Jackie's look.** In this mission he has a shaved head and no cyberware. In every Watson job he has hair, a beard and chrome.
- **V's eyes.** V has no Kiroshi optics here. They arrive in The Ripperdoc, which opens Regina's gigs.
- **The lockdown.** The Rescue ends with Watson sealed until The Heist. If The Rescue is told before this, the story has to say how they got out.
- **T-Bug isn't in this mission.** Everything of hers is a voice we add.

**One correction to our own files:** V doesn't help Pepe for nothing. V asks for a favor in return.

**Not confirmed in the sources read:** that El Coyote Cojo is Mama Welles's bar. The dialogue below leans on it. Check in game.

---

## The idea that makes it work

Pepe pours at Mama Welles's bar, and he owes Kirk. Two people set out to fix it without telling anyone.

- **V** takes Kirk's job as a favor to Pepe, and to find out whether Heywood still knows V.
- **Jackie** goes after the same car, because a debt at his mother's bar is his family's debt, and a legend doesn't ask his friends to cover it.
- **T-Bug** is on both their lines, and doesn't know they're the same job until the garage.

So the gun in the garage is still real. It just belongs to a friend who was too proud to say where he was going.

## The dialogue

### A. Across the water
- **When:** the night after "Cops' Cash." Regina has nothing left for them in Watson.
- **In frame:** V and Jackie, wherever they eat. T-Bug on the call.

> **JACKIE:** So that's Watson. Every roof, every alley.
> **T-BUG:** Good. I liked Watson. I know where everything is in Watson.
> **V:** I've got a thing in Heywood tomorrow.
> **JACKIE:** *(too fast)* Heywood? What thing?
> **V:** Personal. Guy I know's in a hole.
> **JACKIE:** Huh. *(a beat)* Yeah. I got a thing too. Family thing.
> **T-BUG:** Heywood. Across the water. I don't have one camera pinged over there. You both know that, right?
> **V:** It's one night.
> **T-BUG:** It's always one night. Whatever. Not my problem. Cuttin' my wires now. [GAME line]
> *(She doesn't. Both lines stay open.)*

### B. Two years
- **When:** Padre's car. [GAME: Padre welcomes V back after two years, says they could work together again, and gives V his number.]
- **In frame:** V. T-Bug in V's ear, after Padre has gone.

> **T-BUG:** Who's the priest?
> **V:** Padre. Fixer. Runs Heywood.
> **T-BUG:** A fixer who drives you himself. Either you matter or you're bait.
> **V:** He gave me his number.
> **T-BUG:** *(a beat)* A second fixer. Jackie's going to be unbearable.

### C. One car
- **When:** the garage under Embers. [GAME: twenty minutes before the cameras come back. Jackie, gun drawn, says he's taking the car. V says V was there first.]
- **In frame:** the game's own scene. Only T-Bug's voice is added.

> **T-BUG:** *(to V)* Twenty minutes on the cameras. Don't waste them.
> **T-BUG:** *(on her other line)* Twenty minutes. Garage level. The Rayfield.
> *(Silence.)*
> **T-BUG:** *(to both)* ...Which Rayfield.
> *([GAME] The stand-off.)*
> **T-BUG:** Oh, you have got to be kidding me.
> *([GAME] The NCPD arrive. Both lines go to static.)*
> **T-BUG:** Hello? *(a beat)* Hello?

### D. Nothin' personal
- **When:** after they wake on the street. [GAME: they talk through their injuries; Jackie says Kirk doesn't mess with people in Heywood, and suggests lunch.]
- **Where:** a food stall in Heywood (pick in game).
- **In frame:** V and Jackie, both beaten. T-Bug on the call at the end.

> **JACKIE:** *(eating)* Nothin' personal, compa. Just biz. [GAME line]
> **V:** You pulled a gun on me.
> **JACKIE:** I pulled a gun on a guy in a car. Then the guy was you. Then I had a whole situation.
> **V:** You could've put it down.
> **JACKIE:** You could've got out of the car.
> *(They eat.)*
> **V:** Pepe?
> **JACKIE:** Pepe. *(a beat)* He pours at my mother's bar, V. He owes Kirk, that's my family owin' Kirk. I wasn't gonna bring that to the crew.
> **V:** Why not?
> **JACKIE:** 'Cause a legend don't need his friends to cover his mama's bartender.
> **V:** I wasn't going to bring it either.
> **JACKIE:** Yeah? What's your excuse?
> **V:** He asked me. Before I knew he was yours. *(a beat)* And I wanted to see if Heywood still knew me.
> **JACKIE:** *(quiet)* You help my homies, you're OK in my book. No harm, no foul. [GAME line]
> **V:** Won't leave you hangin', Jack. But don't get used to it. [GAME line]
> **JACKIE:** *(grinning through a split lip)* Sense a kind of chemistry, y'know? [GAME line]
> *(The call chimes.)*
> **T-BUG:** Nine hours.
> **JACKIE:** Bug...
> **T-BUG:** Nine hours of static, on two lines. I got to hear a suit tell a cop to break your legs, and then nothing. Do you know what nothing sounds like?
> **V:** We're okay.
> **T-BUG:** I don't care if you're okay. I care that I didn't know. That's different.
> **JACKIE:** That's the same, Bug.
> **T-BUG:** You want nice, supportive? Call a damn helpline. [GAME line] *(a beat)* ...Is the food any good over there?
> **V:** Come and find out.
> **T-BUG:** Across the water. No.
> **JACKIE:** We got a second fixer's number, Bug. Padre. Heywood.
> **T-BUG:** *(a long pause)* So it's started.

## The four moments

The subject of the gig: carrying a debt for somebody else.

- **V:** the one who tells the truth kept a secret, and learns that leaving something out is also a choice about what to show.
- **Jackie:** being a legend and being a son pull in different directions, and the son went out alone with a gun. (His mother's bar; the debt his father never paid for anything.)
- **T-Bug:** the fear comes out plainly for once. Nine hours of static is the thing she built every wall against.
- **The crew:** after a whole act of working together, their first job outside Watson is the one each tried to do alone. They carry each other's debts from here.

---

## Another way to frame it

**It's an act.** The crew agrees never to be seen together across the water, so V and Jackie play strangers for the cameras: the gun, "I was here first," even the introductions afterwards. Nothing has to be cut but Atlanta. The cost is that the stand-off stops being real, and the scene becomes a caper instead of a wound.
