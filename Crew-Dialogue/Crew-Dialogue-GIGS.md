# Crew Dialogue: the gigs

The side content available before The Heist, and the four moments each gig carries. The rule is in `Crew-Dialogue.md`: every gig gives one personal development to each of the three and one bonding moment to the crew, and all four tie to the gig's subject, never to the action or the outcome.

Everything under a gig below is a **seed**: a starting point for dialogue, not the dialogue.

---

## How much there is

Apart from the main jobs, 51 entries are available before The Heist.

| Kind | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gigs | 22 | Regina's Watson gigs |
| Side jobs | 9 | One of them, The Gift, is T-Bug's own |
| Cyberpsycho sightings | 6 | Regina's, in Watson |
| NCPD work | 14 | 11 single jobs, and 3 entries that group 24 "assault in progress" sites |
| **Total** | **51** | |

At four moments each, the 22 gigs alone carry 88 moments. All 51 would carry 204.

---

## The subject

A gig's subject is what the job is about, read from its brief. Woman of La Mancha is about an honest cop who won't drop a case. It is not about how V gets into her room or what happens to her.

Each character meets the subject through their own end:
- **V** through an idea from photojournalism.
- **Jackie** through his family and his father, often touching one of the things that will sit on his ofrenda.
- **T-Bug** through her fear, with a cutting line on top and her care showing underneath.
- **The crew** through one thing the three say or do together.

## The 22 gigs

### 1. Woman of La Mancha
**Subject:** an honest cop who won't drop a case that everyone wants dropped.
- **V:** protect the source. Who does a picture of a whistleblower protect, and who does it expose?
- **Jackie:** stubbornness that gets you killed, and the mother who once made him quit before it did. (The Valentinos.)
- **T-Bug:** "Nobody thanks the honest ones. They bury them." She says only suckers have principles. She has three, and has never broken one.
- **The crew:** each says the one thing they'd never drop.

### 2. Troublesome Neighbors
**Subject:** a neighbor who makes a whole block worse.
- **V:** the establishing shot. A street says who lives on it before any face does.
- **Jackie:** Heywood, where everybody knew whose son he was. A legend looks after his block.
- **T-Bug:** she has no neighbors, on purpose: "Neighbors are witnesses with keys." She still knows which stall the other two eat at.
- **The crew:** the block each would stand up for. T-Bug's answer is a noodle stall.

### 3. Backs Against the Wall
**Subject:** a war veteran stealing the medicine he needs.
- **V:** dignity and consent. How do you photograph someone at their lowest?
- **Jackie:** men who bring the war home with them. (The belt, kept just in case.)
- **T-Bug:** nobody covers you when the policy lapses, and her retirement fund is the only insurance she trusts. She knows both their blood types. "In case I ever need a donor."
- **The crew:** who would come for each of them.

### 4. Flight of the Cheetah
**Subject:** leaving home to stay alive, and trusting strangers to get you out.
- **V:** the portrait of someone leaving. What do they carry?
- **Jackie:** he has never really left Heywood. The furthest he got, he came back with a bottle he's never opened. (The tequila.)
- **T-Bug:** leaving is her whole plan, and strangers are her whole fear: "Nomads. Great. People with no address." Her escape plan has a seat count, and it's more than one.
- **The crew:** where each would go. Nobody believes T-Bug's porch.

### 5. Bloodsport
**Subject:** a coach paying for his fighter's bet.
- **V:** credit. Every picture has people behind it who never get named.
- **Jackie:** a coach is a father you pick. (Sparring with Vik: "an arm like a cannon.")
- **T-Bug:** "I taught myself." She didn't, and she won't say who did. Owing someone frightens her.
- **The crew:** who each would take a fall for.

### 6. Rite of Passage
**Subject:** what people do to belong, and a recording of it.
- **V:** a recording isn't consent. The same tape is proof or a weapon.
- **Jackie:** the Valentinos. What he did to get in, the scar near his heart, and that they let him walk.
- **T-Bug:** she never joined anything: "A club is a list somebody else keeps." She has kept every message the two of them ever sent her.
- **The crew:** this crew had no initiation. They just kept showing up.

### 7. Playing for Keeps
**Subject:** a piece of a body, kept in a jar as property.
- **V:** "taking" a picture. What does a photo take from the person in it, and who owns it after?
- **Jackie:** the body his mother gave him, and how much chrome a legend needs.
- **T-Bug:** being parted out, owned, sold on: "Everything about me that's worth money is already on a server."
- **The crew:** the one part of themselves each wouldn't sell.

### 8. Small Man, Big Evil
**Subject:** a small man with power over people poorer than he is. (Read from the title; check in game.)
- **V:** point the lens up. Photograph power, not poverty.
- **Jackie:** a small man at home with a belt in his hand. (The belt.)
- **T-Bug:** she knows the type from the inside of a locker. She's at her sharpest here, and her most scared.
- **The crew:** who made each of them feel small.

### 9. The Heisenberg Principle
**Subject:** a drug lab under a building where people live. The title's idea: you can't watch a thing without changing it.
- **V:** the observer changes the scene. People act differently once the camera is up.
- **Jackie:** what Heywood's kids get fed, and a mother's bar that feeds the block.
- **T-Bug:** she never takes the edge off: "The edge is what's keeping me alive." She tells them both to eat real food.
- **The crew:** T-Bug is different when V's camera is on her, and Jackie says so.

### 10. Last Login
**Subject:** what a person leaves behind on a machine.
- **V:** the archive. What happens to the pictures after the photographer?
- **Jackie:** a book he keeps meaning to finish. (The book.)
- **T-Bug:** she plans to leave nothing: "When I go, I go clean." She keeps one folder. It's their pictures.
- **The crew:** what each would want found.

### 11. Monster Hunt
**Subject:** who gets called a monster.
- **V:** the caption decides. "Monster" is a caption, and a fair picture of someone you despise is the hardest kind.
- **Jackie:** whether a man is a monster or just a man who did it. (The belt.)
- **T-Bug:** men like this are who she built the walls for. She says she's bored, and stays on the line the whole time.
- **The crew:** what each would do if their own monster came back.

### 12. Shark in the Water
**Subject:** debt, and the man who lives off it.
- **V:** who pays for the picture? V owes for the very eyes V sees with.
- **Jackie:** "We never owed a shark. We owed neighbors." And the one thing you don't sell when you're broke. (The tequila.)
- **T-Bug:** never owes, never lends: "Debt is a leash with math on it." She covered a bill once and called it a rounding error.
- **The crew:** what they owe each other. Nobody's counting, except T-Bug, who has the count.

### 13. Welcome to America, Comrade
**Subject:** being tracked from the moment you arrive.
- **V:** witness and surveillance use the same camera. The difference is consent.
- **Jackie:** a legend is somebody everyone can find. He wants that.
- **T-Bug:** being found is the fear under all her others: "I'm the bug. That's the joke."
- **The crew:** Jackie wants to be found. T-Bug never does. V only afterwards, in the pictures.

### 14. Catch a Tyger's Toe
**Subject:** planting a lie in a machine.
- **V:** never alter the frame. One doctored picture poisons the true ones.
- **Jackie:** "I steal. I don't lie." The difference matters to him, because of who showed him what lying looks like.
- **T-Bug:** the trick isn't even hers: "It's not my code. I just carry it." She's afraid of being found out as ordinary.
- **The crew:** one true thing each.

### 15. Dirty Biz
**Subject:** a recording somebody would kill to get back.
- **V:** the edit. Some pictures shouldn't run, and choosing is the job.
- **Jackie:** what his mother never needs to see.
- **T-Bug:** "I don't watch them. I move them." She tells V not to watch it.
- **The crew:** each has seen something they can't unsee. Nobody says what. They sit with it.

### 16. Hippocratic Oath
**Subject:** a healer who treats whoever is on the table.
- **V:** shoot or help? The photographer's version of the oath.
- **Jackie:** the people who patched him up: Vik, and Misty in her own way. (The mandala.)
- **T-Bug:** who would treat her? No doctor knows her name. She has Vik's number saved "for V, obviously."
- **The crew:** each one's oath.

### 17. Lousy Kleppers
**Subject:** thieves robbing thieves.
- **V:** stealing a picture. Is a shot taken without asking a theft?
- **Jackie:** he and V met stealing the same car. Honor among thieves is the only kind he was raised around.
- **T-Bug:** "I only steal from people who can't find me."
- **The crew:** what each has taken from the others: a word, a habit, a place to eat.

### 18. Many Ways to Skin a Cat
**Subject:** a van full of jackets: what people wear to be somebody.
- **V:** the detail shot. Clothes tell the story before the face does.
- **Jackie:** the look of a legend, and the one thing to wear that he kept from his father. (The belt.)
- **T-Bug:** nobody knows what she wears. "That's the point."
- **The crew:** what each wears as armor.

### 19. Occupational Hazard
**Subject:** a job that breaks the person doing it.
- **V:** what looking does to the one who looks.
- **Jackie:** he knows this work ends one way. He wants out with enough for Mama first.
- **T-Bug:** retirement means getting out before the job breaks her. This is the closest she comes to saying she's afraid. She asks how V sleeps.
- **The crew:** a promise to tell each other when to stop.

### 20. Scrolls Before Swine
**Subject:** footage as leverage, and mercy.
- **V:** evidence and blackmail are the same frame. The use is the difference.
- **Jackie:** his father ran and was never seen again. Was letting him run a mercy?
- **T-Bug:** leverage is the only safety she trusts. She has plenty on both of them and has never used it.
- **The crew:** they admit they all have dirt on each other, and laugh.

### 21. Freedom of the Press
**Subject:** a reporter who has given up, and the mentor who hasn't.
- **V:** does telling the truth change anything? V's own question, said out loud by someone else.
- **Jackie:** a legend is just a story somebody bothered to tell. He asks V to tell his right.
- **T-Bug:** "The truth gets people killed." She listens to Regina's old broadcasts anyway.
- **The crew:** V shows them the first picture with all three in it: two faces and a phone.

### 22. Fixer, Merc, Soldier, Spy
**Subject:** who you really work for.
- **V:** independence. The picture belongs to whoever isn't paying for it.
- **Jackie:** he works for Mama, Misty and Heywood. The fixers just pay.
- **T-Bug:** "I work for me." She has been taking a smaller cut to stay on this crew.
- **The crew:** each names who they work for. T-Bug's answer doesn't match what she does, and nobody points it out.

## Open

- **Which entries get the four moments:** the 22 gigs only, or the side jobs, cyberpsychos and NCPD work too.
- **Gigs whose brief asks for someone to be killed or "taken out":** Troublesome Neighbors, Small Man, Big Evil, Monster Hunt and Shark in the Water. How a pacifist V plays them is undecided.
- **Thin subjects:** Small Man, Big Evil, Catch a Tyger's Toe, Last Login and Lousy Kleppers have one-line briefs in the planning log. Their subjects here are read from titles and need checking in game.
- **The basketball, and what the ofrenda's guests say:** not in the sources found, so Jackie has nothing to reference there yet.
- **The order of the gigs,** which decides which moment comes first for each character.
