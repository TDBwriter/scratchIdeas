Theme branch for narrative playthroughs of Cyberpunk 2077: character concepts, run logs, story beats, and ideas worth pulling into larger works.

This file holds one entry per playthrough, in the template at the bottom. Only settled decisions are written in. Anything not decided is left blank or marked open. ## Playthrough 1: V-media

**V:** V / Streetkid / no build until The Heist, then quickhacks and guns

- **Name, body and voice:** blank. V is kept neutral in every file. The game requires a body and a voice at character creation; neither is picked.
- **Build before The Heist:** all attributes stay at 3 (the 7 creation points were left unspent), and V has only the cyberware the game installs.
- **Build after The Heist:** in response to it, V chromes up. Intelligence to 20 first, then Reflexes and Cool as needed; Body and Technical Ability are dump stats. Best in class for hacking, free-running and brawling; weapon and survival perks only if points are spare. Cyberware chosen for stealth, free-running and reconnaissance. V goes from Streetkid sidekick to a one-person powerhouse, melting brains with quickhacks and stacking bodies like Jackie.
- **Limits:** the base game with Update 2.5, without Phantom Liberty. Level cap 50, 71 attribute points.
- **Tools:** wits, a camera, a tomahawk, fists and a gun. Which gun: blank.
- **Open on the build:** brawling leans on Body, which is a dump stat; the level-by-level build and the matching Cyberpunk RED sheet (Media role), which aren't written.

**Premise:** V-media is a pacifist, free-running, truth-telling documentarian who is more interested in capturing the perfect still frame than the perfect score. V is into street art (graffiti, mixed media, film, music, protest), kindness and community: a street kid just trying to survive, make friends and make art. V publishes a screamsheet, *Cheap Truth*, and runs with a crew of three: Jackie the muscle, T-Bug the brains, V the eyes.

**Key choices:**
- **The run:** hardest difficulty (Very Hard). No mods. Every side job, gig, cyberpsycho sighting and NCPD entry in Watson is done before The Heist.
- **One save.** A second, V-Jackie save for Jackie's fight clips was planned and is dropped. Its build file, `cyberpunk2077JackieBuild.md`, is retired.
- **Two orders:** the game is played in its own order, with The Streetkid first. The videos tell the opening jobs (Practice Makes Perfect, The Rescue, The Ripperdoc), then every side gig, then The Streetkid, then The Ride, The Information, The Pickup and The Heist.
- **Rule of force:** from the first job, V doesn't start violence and doesn't take life wantonly. V answers escalation by the other party with lethal force and goes loud in self-defense when attacked. The tomahawk is for silenced kills if the situation calls for one.
- **Who fights:** every fight on screen is V's footage. Each fight is credited to Jackie or to V in the edit, decided as it's cut.
- **Hacking:** before The Heist, the player performs every hack, the edit covers it with a photo V appears to have snapped, and the story credits T-Bug, who only uses tricks seen in gameplay. After The Heist, V hacks openly as V.
- **New lines:** the crew's invented dialogue is text only. Nobody speaks it.
- **The crew:** no romance. Friendship like brotherhood, and stark differences. T-Bug stays on remote comms wherever the game doesn't put her in the room.
- **The chip:** Jackie slots it after the case is damaged. V slots it after Jackie is shot and dying.
- **Not yet made:** which of the game's dialogue options fit V; the order of The Information and The Pickup.

**Recording so far (November 16, 2026):**
- **Recording has started.** Hours of gameplay are recorded, with Steam's automatic (background) recording.
- **One save.** All the footage is from it. The V-Jackie save is dropped.
- **Played so far:** The Streetkid, Practice Makes Perfect, The Rescue and The Ripperdoc, then NCPD jobs.
- **Viktor is paid back,** out of recovered weapons and ammo.
- **Build so far:** "I have chosen not to level, building skills and equipment where I can." V levels up from XP, and every attribute and perk point is banked, so attributes stay at 3. Skills rise by use.
- **Videos:** every entry is edited into a full video of its own, each assault site included. Before The Heist that is 80: the 72 side entries and the 8 main jobs from The Streetkid to The Heist. A few deaths before the success stay in the full video. Each mission also gets a Short of up to 3 minutes, cut in the style of a 90s skate-video part, with that mission's deaths in it (`cyberpunk2077ShortsTreatment.md`).

**Ending:** the secret ending, a solo raid on Arasaka. After The Heist, V stops holding back and chromes up: quicker to escalate, readier to kill, a one-person powerhouse. How the raid itself is played is open. Nothing past The Heist is planned in detail.

**Notes & threads to keep:**
- **The screamsheet:** `Cheap-Truth-screamsheet/`. A text-only Markdown zine V fictionally publishes. It stands on its own.
- **The crew's storyline:** `Crew-Dialogue/`. Our own scenes and seeds for V, Jackie and T-Bug, compatible with what the game shows. It stands on its own.
- **The shooting plan to The Heist:** `cyberpunk2077PreHeistPlanningLog.pdf`, 61 entries. Its order is a checklist, not the order played (decided: it stays as it is). Its source is in `tools/planning-log/`.
- **Recording and export:** `cyberpunk2077RecordingPipeline.md`.
- **Shorts:** `cyberpunk2077ShortsTreatment.md`.
- **Perks and skills:** `cyberpunk2077PerksAndSkills.md`.
- **Pipeline:** plan, play and record, narrate, publish serially on YouTube. Each mission is a zine issue and an episode. A zine that appears inside the game is a stretch goal only.
